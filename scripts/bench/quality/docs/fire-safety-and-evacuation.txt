Fire safety and evacuation — Acme head office (fictional)

- Assembly point: the park across the street, by the fountain. Do not use lifts.
- Each floor has two fire wardens wearing yellow vests; they check meeting rooms and toilets before leaving.
- Fire extinguishers are next to each stairwell door. Defibrillator (AED): 3rd floor reception.
- Evacuation drills take place twice a year, in April and October.
- Report a fire by calling 119 first, then the building security desk at extension 2400.
- Visitors are the responsibility of the employee who invited them.
