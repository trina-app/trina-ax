# Acme Desk release notes — 2026

## 4.3.0 — 2026-10-14
- Guest passes: hosts can book a desk for a visitor and send a QR code by email.
- Admins can export booking history as CSV.
- Fixed: bookings made across midnight showed the wrong date (ACME-1432).

## 4.2.0 — 2026-08-18
- Floor heatmap shows which desks are used most over the last 30 days.
- Recurring bookings (every weekday, every Monday, ...).
- Fixed: the sensor battery warning appeared twice (ACME-1377).

## 4.1.0 — 2026-05-06
- Single sign-on with OIDC, next to SAML.
- Desk neighbourhoods: reserve a zone of desks for one team.

## 4.0.0 — 2026-02-02
- New booking screen, 40% fewer taps to book a desk.
- Dropped support for Internet Explorer 11.
