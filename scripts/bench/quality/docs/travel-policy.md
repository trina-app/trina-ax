# Business travel policy

Applies to all Acme employees travelling for work. Book through the company travel desk.

## Daily allowance (per diem) and hotel caps
| Destination | Per diem | Hotel cap per night |
|---|---|---|
| Seoul metro area | none | none (no overnight stay) |
| Other Korean cities | 50,000 KRW | 120,000 KRW |
| Japan, China, Southeast Asia | 80 USD | 180 USD |
| North America, Europe | 110 USD | 260 USD |

## Transport
- Trains (KTX) in standard class. Domestic flights only when the train takes more than 4 hours.
- International flights in economy. Premium economy is allowed for flights longer than 9 hours.
- Personal car use is reimbursed at 300 KRW per km, plus tolls.

## Approval and claims
- Trips abroad need approval from a division head at least 14 days before departure.
- Submit receipts in the expense tool within 10 days of returning.
- The per diem covers meals and small costs; do not claim meals separately.
