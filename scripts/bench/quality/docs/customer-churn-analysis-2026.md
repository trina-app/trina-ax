# Customer churn analysis — first half of 2026

Prepared by the Customer Success team. Figures are made up.

## Headline numbers
- Customers at the start of 2026: 640. Customers lost by June 30: 38, a churn rate of 5.9% for the half year.
- Revenue lost to churn: 214 million KRW in annual contract value.
- 29 of the 38 lost customers were on the Starter plan.

## Why customers left
| Reason | Customers | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Price after the renewal increase | 14 | 37% |
| Moved to a competitor with a free tier | 9 | 24% |
| Office closed or downsized | 8 | 21% |
| Missing features (mobile app, Slack) | 5 | 13% |
| Other | 2 | 5% |

## What we will do
- Offer a 2-month discount to Starter customers who reach 80% of their booking limit, before renewal.
- Call every customer whose weekly logins drop by half for three weeks in a row.
- Target: churn below 4% in the second half of 2026.
