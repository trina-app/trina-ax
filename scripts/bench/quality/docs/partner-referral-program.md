# Partner referral program

For resellers and consultants who introduce new customers to Acme Desk. Fictional terms.

- Commission: 15% of the customer's first-year subscription fees, and 5% in the second year.
- Paid every quarter, within 30 days after the quarter ends, once the customer has paid.
- Minimum payout: 500,000 KRW. Smaller amounts roll over to the next quarter.
- A referral counts for 90 days after it is registered in the partner portal.
- No commission for customers who were already in a sales conversation with Acme in the past 6 months.
- Partners who bring 10 or more paying customers in a year move to the Gold tier: 20% in the first year.
