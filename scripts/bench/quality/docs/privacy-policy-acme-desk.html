Acme Desk privacy notice

This notice explains how Acme Corp. (a fictional company) handles personal data in the Acme Desk app. Last updated 2026-08-01.

What we collect

  • Name, work email address and team, from your company's administrator.
  • Desk and room bookings, check-in times and the floor you booked.
  • Device type and app version, for support.

How long we keep it

Booking history is kept for 3 years and then deleted. When your company closes its account, all personal data is deleted within 30 days.

Access logs are kept for 1 year to investigate security incidents.

Who we share it with

Hosting is provided by Bluepeak Example Co. in Seoul. We do not sell personal data and do not use it for advertising.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data through your company's administrator or the privacy team. We answer within 10 days.
