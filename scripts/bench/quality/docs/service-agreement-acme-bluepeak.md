# Master Services Agreement — Acme Corp. and Bluepeak Example Co.

Agreement no. MSA-2026-031. Effective 2026-05-01. All names and figures are fictional.

## 1. Services
Bluepeak Example Co. ("Provider") hosts and operates the Acme Desk booking platform for Acme Corp. ("Customer"),
including monitoring, backups and security patching.

## 2. Service levels
- Monthly uptime target: 99.9%, measured per calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least
  72 hours in advance.
- If uptime falls below 99.9%, Customer receives a service credit of 10% of that month's fee; below 99.0%, 25%.
- Service credits are Customer's sole remedy for missed uptime, and must be claimed within 30 days after the month ends.
- Backups run daily and are kept for 35 days. Restore requests are started within 4 business hours.

## 3. Fees and invoicing
- Monthly fee: 7,800,000 KRW (VAT excluded), invoiced on the first business day of each month.
- Customer pays within 30 days of receiving an invoice.
- Provider may raise the fee once per contract year, by no more than 5%, with 90 days' written notice.

## 4. Term and termination
- Initial term: 36 months. It then renews automatically for successive 12-month periods.
- Either party may end the agreement for convenience with 60 days' written notice after the first 12 months.
- Either party may terminate immediately if the other commits a material breach that is not cured within 20 days of
  notice.

## 5. Data
- Customer data stays Customer's property. Provider processes it only to deliver the services.
- After termination, Provider returns Customer data in CSV and JSON format within 15 days on request, and deletes all
  copies within 30 days.
- Provider reports any personal data breach to Customer within 24 hours of discovering it.

## 6. Liability
Each party's total liability under this agreement is capped at the fees paid in the 12 months before the claim. The cap
does not apply to breaches of confidentiality or data protection obligations.

## 7. Governing law
This agreement is governed by the laws of the Republic of Korea. Disputes go to arbitration at the Korean Commercial
Arbitration Board in Seoul.
