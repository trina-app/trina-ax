MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT (비밀유지계약)
Between Acme Corp. and Northwind Sample Inc. (fictional). Effective 2026-06-10.

1. Confidential information stays confidential for 3 years after the end of the discussions.
   비밀유지 의무는 협의 종료 후 3년간 유지된다.
2. Each side may share confidential information only with employees who need it.
3. On request, all copies must be returned or destroyed within 30 days.
4. Remedy for breach: injunctive relief plus damages. No fixed penalty amount is agreed.
5. Governing law: Korea. Venue: Seoul.
