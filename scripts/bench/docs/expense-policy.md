# Expense policy / 경비 규정

| Item | Limit | Approval |
|---|---|---|
| Team dinner (회식) | 40,000 KRW per person | Team lead |
| Taxi | Receipt needed over 30,000 KRW | None under limit |
| Business flights | Economy only | Director |
| Software subscriptions | Over 100,000 KRW per month | CFO |

Claims must be filed within 30 days of the purchase. 경비 청구는 지출일로부터 30일 이내에 해야 한다.
