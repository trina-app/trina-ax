Information Security Policy / 정보보안 규정

  • Passwords must be at least 14 characters. 비밀번호는 최소 14자 이상.
  • Passwords are rotated every 180 days.
  • Multi-factor authentication is required for all admin accounts.
  • Lost devices must be reported to IT within 2 hours. 분실 시 2시간 이내 신고.
  • Customer data may not be copied to personal cloud storage.
