Remote Work Policy (재택근무 규정)

Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Core hours are 10:00 to 16:00 KST; everyone must be reachable then.
New hires cannot work remotely during their first 30 days.
Home-office equipment allowance: one-time 300,000 KRW (재택 장비 지원금 30만 원).
Remote work from abroad is not allowed without written approval from HR.
