# Competitor roundup

## Sources
- `groups/Marketing/Competitor notes.md`: what the team noted this month.
- The **Price tracker** sheet in Google Drive (folder "Competitor watch"), when Drive is connected.
- No web pages. If a brand is in neither, say it isn't tracked yet and offer to add it to the notes.

## How
1. Table: | Brand | News this month | Price (10 bags) | How it differs from us |
2. Our own product in the last row: Yuzu Ginger, $11.90 for 10 bags, 20% off at launch.
3. Under the table, two or three lines: "Worth trying ourselves".

## Rules
- Only what the notes or the tracker say. No rumours.
- Don't run other companies down.
- Answer in the chat. Save only when asked.
