# November campaign: "Book the desk, skip the guesswork"

Acme is a fictional company. This page is sample data for the demo.

- Owner: Sam Ortiz
- Dates: November 16 to December 13, 2026
- Goal: 120 trial sign-ups from offices of 10 to 200 people (October: 74)
- Product: Acme Desk Pro, first month free for new teams

## Budget
- Campaign budget: $12,000, from the Marketing Q4 budget
  - Ads: $9,000 (search $5,000, LinkedIn $4,000)
  - Content: $3,000 (two short videos, one customer story)

## Channels and timing
| Channel | What | When |
|---|---|---|
| Search ads | "desk booking app", "hot desk software" | Nov 16 to Dec 13 |
| LinkedIn | Two videos for office managers | Nov 18 to Dec 10 |
| Newsletter | Customer story and the free month | Nov 19 and Dec 3 |
| Website | Landing page with a 2-minute product tour | live Nov 16 |

## Message
- One line: "See who is in, book a desk in two taps."
- Proof: Harbor Labs cut empty desks from 40% to 15% in two months (customer story, approved).
- Avoid: "the best", "number one", numbers we cannot show.

## Measures
- Trial sign-ups, cost per sign-up (target under $75), trials that become paid within 30 days.
