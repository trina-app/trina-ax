# Leave and benefits

Acme is a fictional company. This page is sample data for the demo.

## Paid leave

- Everyone gets 20 days of paid leave a year, counted from January 1.
- People who join during the year get 1.5 days for each full month left in the year (joining in November: 1.5 days).
- Ask your team lead in the team chat at least 5 working days before; one or two days can be asked 1 working day before.
- Up to 5 unused days move to the next year. They expire on March 31.

## Sick days

- 8 paid sick days a year. Tell your team lead before 10:00 on the day.
- From the fourth day in a row, a doctor's note is needed.

## Company holidays

- The office is closed from December 24 to January 1. These days are not taken from your paid leave.

## Benefits

- Learning budget: $600 a year for books, courses and conferences. Ask your team lead first.
- Home office: $300 once, in your first three months, for a chair, a screen or a desk.
- Questions about leave or pay: Dana Brooks.
