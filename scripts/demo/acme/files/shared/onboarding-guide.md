# Your first week at Acme

Acme is a fictional company. This page is sample data for the demo.

## Day 1
- Sign in to the team app and read this guide, the leave page (`shared/handbook/leave-and-benefits.md`) and your team's folder.
- Meet your team lead for 30 minutes: what your first month looks like.

## Days 2 to 3
- Read the latest notes of your team's weekly meeting and the tasks with your name.
- Marketing: read `groups/Marketing/brand-voice.md` and the November campaign plan.
- Sales: read the pipeline sheet in `groups/Sales/`.

## Days 4 to 5
- Finish your first small task from the team meeting and show it to your team lead.
- Friday 16:00: a 15-minute check-in with your team lead. Bring one question.

## Who to ask
- Your work: your team lead (Marketing: Sam Ortiz, Sales: Priya Nair).
- Leave, pay, equipment: Dana Brooks.
