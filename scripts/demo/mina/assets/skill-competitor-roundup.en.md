# Competitor roundup

## Sources
- The brands or links the person gives. If none, ask which brands to look at first.
- Public web pages only.

## How
1. Table: | Brand | News this month | Price | How it differs from us | Source |
2. Under the table, two or three lines: "Worth trying ourselves".
3. Mark news older than a month.

## Rules
- No rumours without a source.
- Don't run other companies down.
