# Steepling brand guide (2026)

Steepling is a fictional company made up for this demo. This page is sample content.

## In a word
**Calm and warm.** We don't shout. We talk like a good cup of tea: easy and friendly.

## Colours
| Name | Code | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Leaf green | `#1F4D3A` | main colour: titles, logo, dark backgrounds |
| Cream | `#F6F1E7` | light backgrounds, text on dark backgrounds |
| Yuzu yellow | `#F2B630` | accent, **one spot per screen** (a sale badge or a button) |
| Ink | `#1B1F1C` | body text |

- No red, purple or neon.
- No rainbow or pastel gradients. Backgrounds are a flat colour or a real, natural photo.

## Photos
- Natural light, real kitchens and tables, winter mornings. The pouch is always dark green with the cream label.
- No text inside photos: words are set on top.

## Type
- **Fraunces** (SemiBold, italic for one stressed word) for headlines and the wordmark.
- **Pretendard** for everything else: lines, prices, buttons, labels.
- Headlines at least 3× the body size.

## Banners
1. **One message.** A short title, one or two lines.
2. Reading order: title → one line → sale (yuzu yellow badge) → button.
3. Logo **top left**, at least 48px from the edges.
4. Product big on the right. Text never covers it.
5. Sizes: 1200×628 (web and ads), 1080×1080 (Instagram).

## Voice
- Plain and friendly. One exclamation mark per sentence at most.
- No "best" or "number one". Numbers only when we can back them up.
