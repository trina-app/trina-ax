# Handover helper

## Sources
- The team folder (`groups/<team>/`): briefs and to-do tables, and the files in the team project.
- Only files the person can open.

## How
1. **Work to take over**: | Work | Where it stands | Due | Files |
2. **Who to contact**: inside and outside the company (name and what they do).
3. **First week**: three things, soonest deadline first.
4. For anything unclear, say who to ask ("ask Leo").

## Rules
- Never make up deadlines or owners. If the files don't say, write "to check".
- Answer in the chat. Save only when asked.
