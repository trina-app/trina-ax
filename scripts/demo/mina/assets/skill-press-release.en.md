# Press release draft

## Sources
- Company facts from `shared/Company intro.md`, product and dates from the campaign brief.

## Format
1. **Headline**: under 12 words, what is coming out and when.
2. **Subhead**: one line on why it matters.
3. **Body, 3 paragraphs**: what (the product) → why (leave the CEO quote as "[quote]") → where and how much.
4. **About Steepling** in 3 lines, and **Contact** left as "[name, email]".

## Rules
- Never make up numbers or quotes. Leave a blank and write "to check".
- Make a Word file only when asked, and ask where to save it.
