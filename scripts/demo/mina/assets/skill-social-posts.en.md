# 3 social post options

## Sources
- Follow the voice in `shared/Brand guide.md`.
- For a campaign, take the message and the offer from its brief in `groups/Marketing/`.

## How
1. Three options: **A calm**, **B playful**, **C offer-first**.
2. Each: a first line that stops the scroll, 2–3 lines of body, 3–5 hashtags.
3. Use discounts and dates exactly as the brief says.
4. End with one line per option on when it works best.

## Rules
- No "best" or "number one". One exclamation mark per sentence at most.
- Answer in the chat. Save only when asked.
