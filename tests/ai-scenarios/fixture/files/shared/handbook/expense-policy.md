- Submit expense claims by the 25th of each month. Late claims move to the next month.
- Taxi: allowed after 22:00 or with more than 10 kg of samples. Keep the receipt.
- Client meals: up to 50,000 KRW per person; name the client and the purpose.
- Travel: book trains through the office manager (Minho Park). Hotels up to 120,000 KRW per night.
- Questions: Finance (Junho Jung).
