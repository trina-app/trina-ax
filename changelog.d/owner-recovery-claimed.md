- Hosted teams: a new tenant command, `owner-recovery-claimed` (`mycel-ctl owner-recovery-claimed --json` → `{ ok, cleared }`), lets the hosting service clear the recovery lock that a handover sets once the new owner has claimed recovery. `mycel-ctl reset-link` now exits 3 with `owner_recovery_locked` while that lock holds, so the hosting service can tell it from a failure. `handoverSafe` stays off until the hosting service uses it.
