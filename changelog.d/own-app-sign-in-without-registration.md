- **Sign in with your own app** at services that do not let apps register themselves, such as Google's MCP servers (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets, Chat at `*mcp.googleapis.com/mcp/v1`) and APIs that sign in with OAuth, such as NAVER WORKS (`www.worksapis.com`). In the add or edit dialog of External services, an MCP server's sign-in has **내 앱으로 로그인** (the app's Client ID, Client Secret and scopes), and an API can now sign in instead of using a key, with the app's sign-in and token addresses. The dialog shows the redirect address to register at the service, with a copy button. As a template, the team's app lets each member sign in with their own account; members never see the app or its secret. The secret is stored encrypted and never shown again; a new client ID, secret, address or scope means signing in again (on a template, every member connects again). Google is asked for offline access so the sign-in lasts. An API's token is refreshed when it runs out, also after the service refuses it. Setup steps for Google and NAVER WORKS are in docs/INTEGRATIONS.md "Your own app". New optional `oauthClient` on connections and templates (input, patch and view; the view never has the secret); migration 338 (add-only). In all five languages.
