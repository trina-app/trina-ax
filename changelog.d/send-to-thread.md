- One click sends a side chat's answer where it belongs. Under an answer to a question about a room message, **스레드에 보내기** (Send to thread) puts it in that message's thread at once, and its ▾ offers **대화방에 보내기** (Send to room); a follow-up such as "더 짧게" keeps the thread. Any other answer has **대화방에 보내기**. No room list, search or note any more: once sent, the button becomes "✓ 스레드에 보냈어요 · 보기" (or "대화방에 보냈어요", "앞부분만" when the answer was cut), and **보기** opens the thread at the reply or the room at the message; ✦ brings the side chat back. When some members cannot open a linked file or material the answer used, only the confirm step of the dialog opens, with the thread's message or the room on top. A thread that was deleted meanwhile says so under the answer and offers the room. Older side chats send to the room. The side chat opened as a full chat (↗) starts its 대화방에 보내기 dialog with the side chat's room picked. A question about a message now shows that message as a quote (who, when, its first lines) above the question instead of raw "> …" lines, and a new side chat is named after the question rather than the quote's header; a quick question names it "요약: <the message's first words>". API: `POST /api/chats/:id/messages/:messageId/share-to-room` takes an optional `threadRootId` (a message of that room that can have a thread; otherwise 409 `thread_root`, checked before anything else); followers of the thread are notified as for any reply. No data change. In all five languages.
