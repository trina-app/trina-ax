- Trina also finds documents by what they mean, not only by their words. In the AI's document search and in the Files search box, a question in other words or another language can find the right file; it helps most in small and medium folders. It runs on your own server: the model (about 120 MB) is downloaded once, with checked hashes, and indexing runs in the background at low priority (at most a quarter of one CPU core, paused while anyone waits for an answer, only with 1.5 GB of memory free), so a big folder's first pass can take hours while keyword search keeps working. Results always combine words and meaning. On by default; `"knowledgeGraph": {"meaning": false}` in server.json turns it off, and servers with a hosting plan keep it off unless it is set to `true`. The older `search` tool and `MYCEL_SEMANTIC` are removed: the first start deletes their `semantic.db` and keeps the downloaded model. No database migration.
