The dialog shows the members now, × takes one out (struck through with **되돌리기** until you save) and the search adds one (click it to list everyone you can add); added people are marked **새로**, with their title. The edit dialog no longer holds the members. Adding someone still asks first with what they will see; the same confirm now also comes when saving would add a rule you did not choose on an older project folder (and says how many people stop seeing it). People who cannot change the members read who can ("멤버는 김소라님(만든 사람)이나 관리자가 바꿔요"). "참여 대상" is now called "멤버".
