# Community code of conduct

Trina AX welcomes people with different backgrounds, languages and levels of experience. English and Korean
participation are welcome. This policy applies to the project's issues, pull requests and other project-managed
community spaces, and to people representing the project in public.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful and specific. Critique ideas and code, help others reproduce problems, accept constructive feedback,
and give credit for others' work. Respect privacy and use synthetic data in examples.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexual harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disruption and publishing
someone else's private information are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to **contact@trina.app**. Include links or a description of the incident and any
context you are comfortable sharing. Share only what is necessary; do not put private reports into public issues.

Maintainers review reports and may ask for clarification. Depending on severity and repetition, they may request a
correction, remove content, warn a participant, restrict participation temporarily or ban participation. They should
explain the action privately when practical. Reporters and participants may request reconsideration through the
same address; anyone involved in the complaint should step aside from deciding it when another maintainer is available.

Reports are handled as privately as practical. Information may need to be shared with those responsible for
investigating or acting on a report. Retaliation against a person who reports a concern is not acceptable.
