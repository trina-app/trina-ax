Pretendard

Trina AX ships one font file of Pretendard, unchanged: PretendardVariable.woff2 (version 1.3.9, every weight from
45 to 930 in one variable font). It is the typeface of the promo video templates (server/src/promo-video/): the
team server serves it to the page that plays a promo video in the chat (/vendor/fonts/), and the MP4 render uses the
same file. The repository keeps it in resources/promo-video/fonts/.
