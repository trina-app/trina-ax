Noto Sans CJK

The Trina AX Linux server bundle ships two font files of Noto Sans CJK, unchanged, in app/<version>/fonts/:
NotoSansCJK-Regular.ttc and NotoSansCJK-Bold.ttc (version 2.004). Each is an OpenType collection of the Korean,
Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Hong Kong faces of one weight. The server uses them to draw
Korean, Japanese and Chinese text in the PDF files the AI makes (make_file, server/src/tools/make-file.ts); a PDF
carries only the glyphs it uses.
