## Detailed description

Trina AX is an AI workspace for your team. With this extension, the AI can use your own browser when you ask it to:
open a site, read a page, fill in a form, compare prices, collect a table from a site you are signed in to.

How it works
• Connect once: open your team in a tab, click the extension and press Connect. Your team's page asks you to confirm
  the team and your account. Each browser profile is linked on its own, with a name you choose.
• You ask in the chat, the AI works in the browser. Each task opens its own window in the background, with its tabs in
  a group named after the task. The AI never touches your other windows or tabs.
• You stay in charge. The first time the AI wants to use a site, the chat asks you (Allow once, Always allow, Deny).
  Chrome's "started debugging this browser" bar is shown while it works. Stop any task from the extension's icon or
  from the chat; closing the debugging bar stops everything.

Safety built in
• The AI never types passwords or payment card details; it asks you to.
• It cannot open your team's own pages, sign-in and account-security pages (Google, Microsoft, Apple, GitHub, password
  managers), extension stores, or addresses on your computer.
• Office-network addresses need your separate OK.
• Banking, payment and crypto sites always need your explicit OK for that site.
• The extension never reads your cookies or site storage, and it only connects to your own team's address.

Requires a Trina AX team (hosted or your own server). Free to install.
