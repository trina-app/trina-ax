- **AI apps (Claude, ChatGPT)**: a few addresses sending many requests can no longer stop everyone else from connecting
  for minutes on end. When the server is busy, only the networks that flood are slowed down; a person connecting gets
  through.
- **AI apps**: the page where you allow an app (Claude, ChatGPT) now scrolls, and **Allow and continue** / **Deny** stay
  at the bottom of the window. On a laptop window of 1280×720 or a phone the buttons were out of reach.
