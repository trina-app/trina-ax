- When the AI reads a Word file with tracked changes, it now also sees the text that was deleted, marked as
  `[deleted: …]` next to `[inserted: …]`, with who made each change. It can answer "what was changed?".
- When the AI reads an Excel file, it now also sees its charts: the type, the title, the sheet, and each series with
  the cells it comes from and its values, as it already did for charts in Word and PowerPoint.
