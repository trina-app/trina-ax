- A private channel or a group chat can become a project, with its history: **⋯ › Make it a project**. Its people
  become the project's people, the conversation becomes the project's chat room, and the files sent there move to the
  project's Chat attachments folder. People added to the project later see messages from then on and can open those
  files. A file that not everyone in the conversation could see stays in the conversation, marked "Only in this
  conversation", and the room keeps a Files list for it. Only people who may make team projects see the item.
- In a direct message, **⋯ › New project with {name}** makes a new project for the two of you, with its own chat room.
- Admin notes: making a conversation a project is recorded in the audit log as a new project made from a room (the room
  named by its kind only). Its files move in the background, one at a time; a restart in the middle finishes the move
  with no duplicates.
- Admin notes, going back to an older version: files already moved stay in the project's Chat attachments folder and
  open in Files, but show as unavailable in the room; a move left unfinished resumes after updating again. The two new
  lines in the room read "Something in this conversation changed" there.
