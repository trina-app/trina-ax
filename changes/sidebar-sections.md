- Chats: sort your own sidebar into **sections**, as in Slack. Make one from the Channels **+** › New section and pick
  its conversations right away; channels, DMs and group DMs all go in. Move a conversation with its **⋯** › Move to
  section (also in the phone's long-press menu and the conversation's own menu), or drag it in, between sections and
  back out onto Channels or Direct messages, with Undo. An empty section says how to fill it. Only you see your
  sections. The team categories that admins made (added in 0.5.23) are gone; make the same grouping again as a section.
