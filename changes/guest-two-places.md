- A guest can now be in one group chat **and** one project at the same time. Add a guest from a group chat to a project (or the other way round) from the project's **Members** or the chat's **Invite**. A second group chat or a second project is still refused, with the words "A guest can be in one group chat and one project. To add them to more, make them a team member." A guest in both sees and can message the people of both, and nobody else. Out of one place they stay signed in; out of both, they are switched off. Admin › Guests shows both places.
