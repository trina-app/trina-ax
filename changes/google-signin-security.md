- Sign in with Google is safer at a shared or unlocked computer:
  - An account made with Google (no password) can't switch to another Google account by itself. An admin gives a
    temporary password instead.
  - Confirming with Google (instead of a password) now asks Google for a fresh sign-in, not just the Google account
    already open in the browser.
  - Asking for your own account to be deleted without a password always needs that Google confirmation, in a web
    browser.
  - Owners and admins see in Activity whenever someone's Google sign-in is connected, replaced or disconnected, or a
    first password is set.
  - Disconnecting Google, by you or an admin, signs that account out on its other devices.
  - Many sign-in attempts from a few addresses can no longer block people who sign in only with Google.
