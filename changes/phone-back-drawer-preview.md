- On a phone (and in the Android app), Back now closes the open side menu and a full-screen file preview, artifact or
  browser panel, and stays on the page. Before, Back with the menu open left the app, and Back with a file preview open
  changed the folder behind the preview instead of closing it.
- After you open a page from the side menu, one Back now goes to the page before it. One press could do nothing.
