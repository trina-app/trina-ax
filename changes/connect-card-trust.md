- When the AI offers to connect a service you know, such as Notion, the card in the chat now always uses that
  service's official address and says "Official Notion address". An address the AI made up is never used for it. A
  service the app does not know shows its address in bold with a plain warning, and a card that calls itself a known
  service at another address offers no sign-in button.
- Asking the AI to connect a service you already have no longer makes a second connection: if it works, the AI says so;
  if it needs you to sign in again or a new key, the card fixes the connection you have ("Sign in to Notion again").
- Clearer words when you connect your own account to a service your team set up, and for the official address of a
  known service, in all five languages.
