- A company on Google Workspace can have Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs and Google Sheets sign in
  with its own Google app, on a hosted team too. An admin sets it once in **Team settings › Google sign-in** on any
  Google page (**Use your company's app**); people then see the company's name at Google, with no "unverified app"
  warning and no 100-person limit, and connect Google again once. **Use Trina's Google sign-in again** switches back.
  Guide: "Use your company's own Google app" in the docs.
