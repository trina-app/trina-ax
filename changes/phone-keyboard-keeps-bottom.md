- Phones: opening the keyboard at the bottom of a room, DM, thread, side chat or Trina chat keeps the newest message right above the message box (in the iPhone app it stayed where it was and the newest messages went behind the box); scrolled up, the message you are reading stays in place. A long press on a message while typing now takes the keyboard down and shows its sheet, with your draft kept. While you type, the message box sits right on the keyboard (the tab bar steps aside). Sheets with a text field, such as "Set your status", stay above the keyboard with their buttons in sight, and "Set your status" no longer opens the keyboard by itself on a phone.
