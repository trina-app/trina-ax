- **Folder access now adds up like Google Drive.** What a folder gives comes on top of what the folders above give, and a folder can be **limited** so nothing from above reaches it (only the owner and admins, and the people and groups added there, open it). "No access" rules are gone; a person who left a project stays out of it. Existing folders are converted once at the update so nobody gets more access than before: some folders become limited, and where a group used to be cut down, the people it covered are listed by name (Admin shows them). People listed that way keep that access if they later leave the group, and the group screens say so. In the Share dialog this is **Settings › Limit access**, and a limited folder lists who it keeps out under **Access removed**, with Add. **This update cannot be rolled back automatically.** Apps connected over MCP may say "Access changed" once.
