- Asking Trina to put a file in a room now sends the file itself. It used to paste the file's text into the message.
- When you say "that thread" and Trina can't tell which one, it asks and shows two or three to pick from. It no longer
  guesses.
- When Trina can't share something, it says why and what to do next: whose file it is and an offer to ask them, who can
  turn sharing back on, or "I can't share the whole folder yet, but I can share the 3 files in it". If the person can
  already open the file, it says so.
- Cards no longer show folder paths. Files are listed by place and name ("My folder › Work › Quote.pdf"), so two files
  with the same name can be told apart, and choices Trina offers read the same way.
- The share card says "it" for one file, and the title of a thread reply card no longer shows `**` marks.
- When a share or post card shows up although you chose **Always allow**, the card now says why (Trina read something
  other people wrote in this chat) and how to skip it: name the file and who gets it in your message.
