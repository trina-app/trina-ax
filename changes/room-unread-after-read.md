- A conversation you read to the end is no longer bold in the list when someone replied in a thread you follow. As in
  Slack, the row shows the conversation's own messages; new thread replies show under their message, in the
  conversation's thread button and in **Mentions & threads** (a reply that mentions you still shows "@").
- The number on the app icon, the Dock and the browser tab now counts the same conversations as the list: a reply in a
  thread you follow adds to it only when it mentions you.
