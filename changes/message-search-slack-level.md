- **Finding messages** (search, ⌘K): a Korean word finds it with any particle after it ("회의를" finds "회의") and an
  English word its other forms ("deploys" finds "deployed"). Put a phrase in quotes to find it exactly, and narrow with
  `from:` a person (`from:me`) or `in:` a channel or a direct message (`in:#marketing`, `in:@name`). Messages are shown
  best match first (or newest first, from the menu above them), and a typing mistake still finds close matches.
