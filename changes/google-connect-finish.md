- Connecting Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs or Google Sheets from the desktop app now finishes in the app, as
  Google Drive does: the browser page that Google opens says "Go back to Trina AX to finish" and opens the app. Before,
  that page always said "This browser can't finish it".
- The page Google opens after a sign-in no longer says "This sign-in has expired or was already used" when the
  sign-in worked. If you reload it, open it twice, or it is from an older try and the connection was made since, it
  says the sign-in worked or "Connected. You can close this window". For a sign-in from the desktop app, the page
  opens the app by itself, and the app's wait line says to press "Back to the app" if it does not.
