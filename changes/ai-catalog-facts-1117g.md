- Asked to change a team skill they can't edit, the AI no longer tells a member to press "Suggest as skill" (that
  button suggests a new skill, it can't change an existing one). It offers to write a short message for the people
  who manage team skills, or a personal copy.
- Asked to use Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs or Google Sheets before they are connected, the AI points to the
  app's own page in Settings › Connectors (or says plainly that it can't be connected on this team's app yet) instead
  of searching the web and citing developer pages, and it asks nothing about the task until it is connected.
- The AI answers in the language a message is written in, even when the person's app is set to another language.
- Owners and admins asking whether a guest can use AI are told where the setting is (Members & invites › Guests, at
  invite or on the guest's row), and on an install where guests can't have AI, that only "No AI" can be picked there.
- Asked to sum up the comments under its answers in a shared chat when there are none, the AI says nobody has replied
  yet instead of suggesting they may show up later.
