- The app's translations now use one voice and one word per thing. Spanish always speaks to you as *usted* and says
  *este ordenador* for your computer (it said *este equipo*, which also means "this team"). Indonesian always uses
  *Anda*. Korean calls people groups **부서** everywhere (그룹 is left only in 그룹 대화). Japanese calls group chats
  **グループトーク**, like チームトーク and 1:1トーク. Simplified Chinese uses 小组 for groups, 聊天室 for rooms and 群聊 for
  group chats throughout. The user guides in these languages quote the new labels.
