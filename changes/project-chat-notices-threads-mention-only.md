- A team project's chat now tells its people about a new message from a teammate, as a chat shared with people does: an
  unread number on the chat and on Projects, the app icon, a phone notification and, in the desktop app, a notification
  with the conversation sound. Trina's answers only add to the number. "Mute this chat" and "@" mentions work there too.
- Thread replies no longer notify you unless they mention you, in conversations, shared chats and project chats. They
  still show in Mentions & threads. To be notified of every reply again, turn on "Replies in threads I'm part of" in
  Settings › Notifications.
