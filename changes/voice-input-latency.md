- Voice input in a chat now shows your words while you keep talking: a stretch of speech is written down after about 7
  seconds at most, or at a short pause (0.6 s), instead of waiting up to 15 seconds or a full second of quiet. With
  background noise this helps the most. The first words still show within about 3 seconds.
- Voice input no longer writes a row of one strange letter (such as "ლლლლლ") when it only heard a fan or a room's hum.
