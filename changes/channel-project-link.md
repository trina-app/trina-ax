- **A channel for a project.** A team project's page has a **Channel** box: **Create a channel** makes a private channel
  with the project's people already ticked, and **Link a channel** links one you have (or link it from the channel's info,
  **Link to a project**). In a linked channel, **Files** at the top opens the project's folder, a document or picture
  sent there has **Save to project** (one click, with **Undo**), and Trina in the channel's side chat saves new documents
  in the project's folder. Only people who can open the project see its name; the others see that the channel was linked
  to a project. Members of the channel and of the project stay separate.
