For the owner and admins, a project's 멤버 row says "멤버 3명" (it said "5명이 봐요", counting the owner and admins, who see every project by role); the members dialog and the new-project line say "멤버 3명 · 팀장·관리자 2명도 볼 수 있어요"; a folder's chip in Files says "3명" and its panel "3명이 봐요" with "팀장·관리자 2명도 볼 수 있어요" under it; the share switch says "멤버 3명과 팀장·관리자 2명". Someone who is both a member and an admin counts once, as a member. A member's new-project line says "멤버 2명 · 팀장·관리자도 볼 수 있어요", without a number for the owner and admins; a member's other counts are as before. Nobody's name is shown for the owner and admins. The API sends the count apart (`split`, `audienceSplit`: `direct` and `byRole`) to the owner and admins only.
