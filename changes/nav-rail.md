- **New navigation.** On a computer, a narrow bar on the left has four places, each with its name: **Trina** (your chats
  with the AI), **Chats** (team rooms, group conversations and direct messages), **Files** and **Activity**. The list of the place you
  pick shows beside it, one list at a time. Your photo at the bottom of the bar has your status, Settings and Sign out;
  for owners and admins, the team's logo at the top has Invite people and Admin.
- On a phone the tabs are now **Trina · Chats · Files · Me**. The ☰ menu is gone: your Trina chats are the Trina tab,
  with **Ask Trina** at the bottom right. Projects are in the Trina list; the team folder is under Files; Activity is in Me.
- The Trina list shows your pinned chats, your folders, the projects you used lately (more behind **All projects**) and
  your other chats, then Scheduled, Team skills and Deleted history. There is no five-chat limit any more.
- **Folders** for your own lists: make one with **+**, then move a chat or a conversation in from its **···** menu or by
  dragging it. Only you see your folders; they change nothing about who can see a chat. **Trina folders** hold Trina
  chats and **Chat folders** hold conversations; each list says which kind it shows.
- When a Trina chat waits for your answer, **Waiting for you** sits on top of the Trina list, and **Trina** on the bar
  opens that chat. Scheduled, Team skills and Deleted history stay under the list on a computer.
- **Switch team…** under the team's logo in the desktop app, and in **Me** in the phone app.
- **Activity** holds the app's own notices (scheduled task results, things that wait for you); mentions and thread
  replies are under Chats. The Chats list shows every conversation, by kind, in the same order on a phone, and the Chats
  number is the same on both: how many conversations have something unread.
- Your usage is now under **Settings › Usage**. "My folder" is now called **My files**.
