- **Copy link in the Share dialog of a chat and a project.** The chat's Share dialog and the project's Members dialog
  now have **Copy link** at the bottom left, as the file's Share dialog has. After you share, the message that says so
  offers **Copy link** too, so you can share and then send the link in one go. The link opens only for people who can
  already see the chat or project; anyone else gets the "can't be opened" page with nothing of it on it. While people you
  added are not saved yet, one line says they can open the link after you press **Share** (or **Save**).
