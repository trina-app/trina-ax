- **Always allow this site** replaces "Allow this site in this conversation" on the web address card: once you pick
  it, Trina opens other addresses on that site without asking in all your chats and your scheduled tasks. In a shared
  chat with a guest, the choice still counts for that chat only.
- Settings › Allowed for Trina now lists the sites you always allowed, both web sites and office-network addresses
  from the in-app browser, and you can remove each one.
- Sharing files, sharing a folder and posting to a conversation no longer ask again in shared chats when you chose
  **Always allow**, also when your message did not name who gets it, when the post mentions @everyone, and in your
  scheduled tasks in a chat that teammates wrote in.
- A dev environment command that can use a login, after teammates changed files, now offers **Always allow** on its
  card. You can remove it in Settings › Allowed for Trina.
