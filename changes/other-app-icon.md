- An **Other app** in a conversation now shows the first letter of its name on its own colour instead of the same grey
  icon for every Other app. **Change icon** in its settings sets an image (PNG, JPEG or WebP) or an emoji; **Remove
  icon** brings the letter back. The people who manage the conversation's apps can change it. Services such as Sentry
  or Notion keep their logos, and a message sent to the Webhook URL can't change the app's name or icon.
