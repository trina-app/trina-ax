- Disconnecting Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs or Google Sheets on a server that signs in through
  Trina's hosting service no longer removes "Trina AX" from your Google account. That removal used to end the same
  Google account's connections on your other Trina servers and teams too. Now only this server forgets the sign-in,
  and the page offers a link to Google's own page if you want to remove Trina's access everywhere.
- Connecting your own Google Drive or Google app in a browser, with your own Google address, now finishes in the tab
  Google opened: it says "Connected", and you can close it. Before, it said to go back to "the app" and press "Connect
  this account" in the first tab. Another Google address, a team Drive or a team account still asks you to confirm the
  account, and that page now says to go back to the first tab.
