- A chat outside projects can now be shared with chosen people and groups (the server side; the Share dialog follows). Everyone it is shared with sees the whole chat in their own list and may continue it, each question answered with their own access and the shared chat's rules (no memories, the read card first). Its creator alone changes who sees it; adding people first checks what they would start seeing; removing someone stops their running answer and takes the chat away at once. People it is shared with get a note in Activity and can hide it from their list.
- Searching without a folder in such a chat only looks where everyone in it may read; with no such folder the AI is asked to name one.
