- In a shared chat (a project's chat, or one shared with people) the AI can now use connected services. A team
  connection everyone in the chat already has works as usual. Your own connections work too: before anything is
  fetched, a card that only you see asks whether what the service returns may be shown to the people in the chat
  (Allow once, Always in this chat, Cancel). Team connections stay off while a guest is in the chat, and connecting a
  new service is still done in Settings › Connectors or in a chat of your own.
