- Named group chats are now **private channels**: they are listed under **Channels** with a lock, and group chats
  without a name stay in **Direct messages**, titled by their people. **Channels** has a **+** to create a channel
  (a name is required) and **Direct messages** has a **+** for a **New message** to one or more people. Starting a
  message with the same people as an existing group DM opens that one. A group DM's **···** can turn it into a private
  channel by giving it a name.
