- **Activity** is now the one place for your notices, as in Slack. Mentions and new replies in threads you follow are
  listed there with the app's own notices. On a computer you can filter them with **All · DMs · Mentions · Threads ·
  Trina · Requests**. **Mark all as read** works under All, DMs, Mentions and Threads.
- **Chats** no longer has a **Mentions & threads** row, on a computer or a phone. Old links to that page open Activity
  on the same tab.
- The Activity count, on the bar on the left and on a phone's **Me** tab, now includes mentions and thread replies.
  The **Chats** count still shows how many conversations have something unread.
