- **Company profile decks look like a real company deck.**
  - The company's own photos from the brand images folder now appear on more slides: behind the numbers, and beside
    the "about" and the closing slides. Before, they were used only on the cover.
  - With no photos, bold colour blocks and a large letter from the company name carry the design.
  - Titles and text are larger, and slides no longer leave half the page empty.
  - Process steps sit on one line instead of arrows.
  - The example under 예시 is new, made on an invented office plant company with free stock photos. Every app
    language now has its own example: Japanese, Chinese, Spanish and Indonesian teams see one in their language
    instead of the English one.
  - Japanese and Chinese decks now break lines between characters, keep punctuation off the start of a line, and use
    Japanese or Chinese fonts in the PDF and PowerPoint files.
