- **Delete account** in Settings › Profile now deletes your account by itself 7 days after you ask, with your
  password. The page shows the date, what is deleted, what moves to the team owner's archive and what stays with the
  team, and you can keep your account until then. The team owner and the admins see the date and can delete it sooner.
- When you asked for your account to be deleted, your private chats, memories, trashed files and file history go at
  the deletion, not days later. Sign-ins, phone notifications, schedules and other per-person settings of a deleted
  account are removed too, also for accounts deleted before this update.
- Restoring a backup now reminds you that accounts deleted since the backup come back and must be deleted again.
