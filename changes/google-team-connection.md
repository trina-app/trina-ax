- Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs and Google Sheets can now be shared with the team: an owner or admin connects one
  Google account once (Admin › Connectors › the app › Team settings › **Shared Google account**) and picks who may use
  it, and those people use it in their chats without connecting their own. Only owners and admins can connect, change
  or remove it; members see "Shared by an admin" on the app's page and can still connect their own account. Changes
  such as sending a mail still ask first.
