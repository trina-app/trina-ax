- **Connectors** (was "Connected apps") is simpler: one **Your connectors** list, one line each with one status
  (Connected, Reconnect, Needs attention, or Connect for something your team shared), and **Discover** with only what you
  have not connected yet. Errors, "Run without asking" and the other settings are on each connector's own page. It is
  in the sidebar only: the Connectors tab in Settings is gone, and old Settings links still open the page.
- An app your team has not set up yet still shows at the end of Discover for members, as "Not set up yet" ("Asked"
  once you asked an admin from its page). The AI's answers, scheduled-task notices and the Slack link message now send
  you to **Connectors** instead of "Settings › Connected apps".
