- Sharing a chat takes one press. While you add people or groups, the Share dialog shows what they would start
  seeing, right away and only when something is at stake: "2 answers may have used your personal memories", "3 answers
  used files Mina can't open", older answers that may have used your personal accounts, or team chat rooms they may not
  be in. **Show answers** lists those answers. When nothing is at stake there is no warning, at most one grey line. The
  **Share anyway** step is gone: the button stays **Share**.
- Sharing a private chat with a team project's members says the same specific things instead of "Not everything this
  chat used is known".
- A project's **Members** dialog shows what the people you add will see right under the count, and **Save** saves in
  one press. It still asks first when people lose access or when the save writes a rule you didn't choose.
