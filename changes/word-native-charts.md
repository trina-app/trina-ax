- A chart in a Word report or PowerPoint deck Trina makes is now a real chart you can edit (right-click > **Edit
  Data**), in any language and without asking for "editable". Before, a plain request such as "a Word report with a
  chart" could get a picture of the chart, and the answer did not say so. When a chart has to be a picture, Trina now
  says that its numbers cannot be edited.
