- **Continue in the desktop app after setup.** Right after making the team in a Mac or Windows browser, the owner is
  offered **Continue in the Mac app** (or Windows app). The button opens the app on the team already signed in, with no
  second password, through a one-time link that lasts 3 minutes. Without the app, the page says to download and install
  it, then press the button again. **Continue in the browser** goes on to the welcome steps as before. The link works
  only while that browser stays signed in, and never signs out someone else who is already signed in to the app.
- In a computer's browser, the welcome **Team files** step and **My files** say in one line that finding work files on
  the computer works in the Mac and Windows app, with a link to open it.
- **Settings › Apps** now sends Macs to the download page (the signed app that updates itself and can find work files),
  not to the Mac App Store.
