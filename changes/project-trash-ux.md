- A team project moved to the trash keeps its card for its members, marked **In trash**. Its page says who moved it
  there and when, and that an admin or its creator can restore it; its files, chats and instructions stay closed until
  then. A link to one of its shared chats says the same instead of "This chat can't be opened".
- A project's creator can now restore their own project from the trash. Admins and the creator find it in
  **Files › Trash** as one row with the project's name; the separate "in the trash" cards on the Projects page are gone.
- **Delete for good** on a project now says that its shared chats go to their creators' **Deleted history**.
- The project menu item that moves a team project to the trash now says **Move to trash**.
- A project page where you can't ask (a project you're not a member of, or one in the trash) no longer shows an empty
  **Trina history** with "Ask here".
