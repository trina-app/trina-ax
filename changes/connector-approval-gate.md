- **Safer connected apps:** once a chat has read something from outside (a web page, someone else's file, a service's
  answer), the AI asks before it changes or sends anything through any connected app, even one set to "Run without
  asking" or "Always allow". The card says why. Reading is unchanged.
- The approval card for every connected app now offers **Don't send**, **Send once** and **Always allow**. Always allow
  is your own choice: for a team's app it applies to you only, and "Ask every time" on the app's row turns it back.
- When your own connected app needs a new sign-in or key, the chat shows a **Sign in again** card instead of only text,
  and the AI carries on after you sign in.
