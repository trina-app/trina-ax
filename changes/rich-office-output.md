- Word files the AI makes can now have coloured and highlighted text, a header and footer, page numbers, landscape pages
  and custom margins. Excel files can have conditional formatting, number formats, column widths, frozen rows or
  columns, and the filter turned off.
- The AI can now make a chart, Gantt chart or diagram as a PNG picture, and put a chart into a Word or PowerPoint file.
  Before, it could only draw these as SVG.
