- **Add an app for the team** (Admin › Connectors) now starts with a list of known apps, with their icons and a search;
  picking one fills in its name, address and sign-in, so you only confirm and sign in. **Custom app (enter an address)**
  is the last choice.
- The add-app dialog reads like Team settings: each field with one short line and its box, select or switch on the
  right, normal-size text, and no red errors on a fresh form until you save or leave a field you typed in. Who it is for
  and the sign-in note share one quiet line, **Change** opens who can use it right there (it is no longer repeated inside
  Advanced), and **Advanced** is just the word, with plain selects instead of big option buttons.
- Adding a listed app shows its icon next to "Add Notion" and its address as one small line.
