- An account made with Google has no password. Where others type their password again (handing over the team,
  updates and restores, the team API engine, voice input, removing a member, asking to delete your account), it shows
  **Confirm with Google**: Google opens in a new window, and the step goes on once Google confirms it's you. This works
  in a web browser; in the desktop and phone apps, asking to delete your account works within 10 minutes of signing in.
- Settings › Security & devices has **Set a password** for an account made with Google. Until then, Google can't be
  disconnected from it, by you or an admin; an admin's temporary password still works and disconnects Google.
- Turning off Sign in with Google in Admin › Team settings now only stops new people connecting or joining with Google.
  People without a password still sign in with it, and the server's own Google app can't be removed or swapped while
  they need it.
