- **Allow all websites now covers the AI's web reads.** Turn it on in Settings › Browser, or pick "Allow all websites (turn
  off in Settings)" on an address card. When you ask, the AI opens public websites without asking, in your chats and in
  rooms. It is always your own switch, never another member's. Scheduled tasks still ask. Addresses with long hard-to-read
  strings, long names or long queries still ask too. Both places say what it risks: a hidden instruction in a document,
  page or room message can make the AI send anything it can read to any site. In the desktop app the same switch covers
  the in-app browser, which asks once more in its own window.
- Admins decide with a new line in Team settings, "Also for web pages the AI opens", under "Members can turn on 'Allow all
  websites'". It is on by default. Turning it off turns everyone's switch off, and the activity log records it.
- An address card about a link that repeats what the AI sent now asks even on a site you allowed for the conversation.
