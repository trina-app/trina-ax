- Files: closing a file you are editing (✕, Back on a phone, Esc), opening another file or leaving the page now asks "Discard your changes?" first when you have edits that are not saved; "Cancel" keeps you in the editor with what you typed. Before, only the editor's own Cancel button asked and the other ways threw the edits away silently. Reloading the page or closing the tab shows the browser's own question while edits are not saved.
