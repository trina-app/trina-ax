- Desktop app: Trina's browser keeps working after a sign-out, or when someone else signs in to the same team on the computer. At that moment the app clears the in-app browser's data (the previous person's cookies, site data, cache and logins), and on a busy Mac that clear could never finish: every later browser step of Trina waited forever, removing a team from the app's team list never finished, and the app could not quit. The clear now names exactly what it removes, which finishes in a second or two, and a clear that takes longer than 10 seconds no longer holds anything up. Ships with the next desktop release. No API or data change.
