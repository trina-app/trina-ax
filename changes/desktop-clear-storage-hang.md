- Desktop app: Trina's browser keeps working after you sign out, or when someone else signs in to the same team on the computer. At that moment the app clears the browser's data of the person before (their site logins, cookies and saved site data). On a very busy Mac that clearing could hang, and then Trina could not use the browser again, removing a team from the app's team list never finished, and the app could not quit. The app now clears only the kinds of data a site keeps for a login, which takes a second or two, and if clearing ever takes more than 10 seconds, Trina carries on. A removed team now leaves the list at once. Ships with the next desktop release. No API or data change.
