- When the team's disk is 80% full, and again at 95%, the owner and admins now find a notice in **Activity** that opens
  **Admin › System › Storage** and says how much the files in conversations take. Before, only a pop-up reached the
  people who were online at that moment.
- A file sent in a conversation that does not fit on the team's disk now says who can help. Members and guests read
  "There isn't enough space on the team's storage for this file. Ask an admin to free some space." (no longer "Empty the
  trash", which they could not do); the owner and admins get **See what uses the space**.
