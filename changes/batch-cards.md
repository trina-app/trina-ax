- When one answer asks several things of the same kind (three skills to the team, two room posts, several shares), you
  now get **one card**: what happens to how many, each item on its own line with a tick, and one set of buttons. One
  press does them all, also when you press it hours later; untick an item to leave it out.
- **Always allow** on one card now covers every other open card of the same kind from that answer: they run at once, no
  further asks.
- The skill card leads in plain words: each skill's name and description and one line for who follows it ("팀 모두의
  AI가 이 스킬을 따르게 돼요 (12명)"). Paths, names, SKILL.md and the files (each opens on its own) are behind
  **자세히**.
- A large skill (many reference files) is no longer refused because "the card cannot hold it": it moves on one card,
  or with no card under Always allow.
