- New `demo-gate/` service for the free-roam demo at demo.trina.app. It shows a landing page with a browser check and a Start button, gives each visitor their own warm copy of the demo team and signs them in as Mina. It is also the AI gateway for those copies: it holds the only AI credential and turns every AI error into "The demo is busy, try again in a bit." It has per-visitor start limits, a daily AI cap and a PAUSE kill switch. Not deployed yet.
- demo-gate hardening: copies run as their own user (the gate refuses to start otherwise), each copy is checked to be a fenced free-roam demo before a visitor gets it, and the gate holds each copy to its AI limit, two AI requests at a time and 1 GB of disk. Requests for another model are sent as Sonnet. Visitors on IPv6 are counted by their full /64. Leftover processes are ended after a crash.
