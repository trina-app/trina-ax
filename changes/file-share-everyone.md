- You can share a file with **Everyone on the team**: it is the first choice in a file's **Share** and in the last step
  of **Find work files on this computer**. A line says before you press **Share** that everyone on the team, also
  people who join later, will be able to open it. Guests never can.
- Sharing several files at once now sends each person **one** notice, "Mina shared 5 files with you", which opens the
  list of those files. People you add by name also get a phone notification.
