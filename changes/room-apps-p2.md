- Add an app to any room from its ⋯ menu: pick Sentry, GitHub, Google Forms or another app, copy the address and follow the short guide; it shows live when the first message arrives (or why one wasn't shown). Updates appear as cards with an "App" tag, repeats go into one thread, and the side chat offers "What happened today?" and "Summarise this thread". Each app has its own settings (what to show, Quiet, a new address, Remove), and a paused app can be resumed from Activity.
- Activity tells the people who manage an app when someone who left the room still knows its address, with a way to get a new one.
