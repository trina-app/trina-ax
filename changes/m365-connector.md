- **Microsoft 365 connector.** Settings › Connectors › Discover now lists Microsoft 365: press Connect, sign in with your Microsoft work or school account, and the AI can search and read your Outlook mail, write drafts and send mail, list and create calendar events, find, read and upload OneDrive and SharePoint files, and read and post in your Teams chats. Sending, creating and posting ask first on a card (unless you choose Always allow), like every connector. An admin can also connect one account for the team. It signs in from the desktop app on Windows and Mac and from the web; a server without the Microsoft app says "Not set up yet".
