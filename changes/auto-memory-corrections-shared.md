- **Trina now remembers your corrections, also in shared and project chats.** When you correct a choice it made (the
  wrong account, browser profile, folder or format), it keeps the lesson on its own with a small "Remembered" step you
  can undo, without asking. In a chat other people share, what you say is kept in your own memory only; what others
  write there never becomes your memory, and your private memories are never used there. Trina no longer tells you to
  move to a private chat to save something, and no longer says "next time I will…" without actually keeping it.
