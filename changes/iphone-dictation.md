- **Voice input on the iPhone** (phone app 1.0.10, App Store; not submitted yet): the iPhone itself writes the words
  in the message box while you talk, with Apple's speech recognition on the phone, as the Mac app does. The recording
  never leaves the phone, so it works in confidential chats too. The first use of a language fetches Apple's model
  (that one recording goes through the team server). Older iPhones, a language the phone lacks and Android keep using
  the team server.
