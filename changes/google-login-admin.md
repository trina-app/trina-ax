- Admin › Team settings › Members and AI has **Sign in with Google**, on by default where the server has a Google app.
  Turned off, the sign-in page has no Google button and nobody can connect one; the Google accounts people connected
  stay and work again when it is turned back on. Without a Google app the row says where to set one up.
- Admin › Members & invites shows **Google** beside each person who connected a Google account, and the person's **⋯**
  has **Disconnect Google** (the usual rules: never the owner, and only the owner for admins).
- Activity now tells you when a Google account is connected to your account. If it wasn't you, the line opens Security
  & devices, where you disconnect it and change your password.
- Once you connected a Google account, connecting Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Docs or Sheets picks the same Google
  account without Google's account list, and Security & devices offers Google Drive and Gmail right after you connect.
- New docs page: [Sign in with Google](https://ax.trina.app/docs/use/sign-in-with-google/).
