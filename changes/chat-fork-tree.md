- **Continue any chat of yours in a new chat.** "새 채팅에서 이어 하기" / "Continue in a new chat" in a chat's title menu
  and in its row's "···" makes a new chat only you see with the conversation so far, named "<title> (continued)", and the
  AI goes on from where it was. On a shared chat the item is "Continue in a new chat only you see". The line under the
  box says it is only yours, where it came from (a link), and that the original is unchanged.
- **In the Trina history** such a chat sits indented right under the chat it came from (one level, nothing to fold);
  older chats keep their place. "Move out on its own" gives it a row of its own. Deleting or hiding a chat never
  removes the ones continued from it. A project's "chats only you see" notice no longer counts them.
