- An answer sent to a room (대화에 보내기, or Trina posting it for you) now carries what the answer made beside the chat:
  a promo video goes as its video file (or as its page where the server cannot make the video file), a page or picture
  as its file, and everyone in the room can open and play it. Before, only the text arrived. If the file cannot be
  attached, nothing is sent and you are told why.
- The room copy of an answer no longer says "only I can open this file": that was true in your chat, not in the room.
- With a thread or the side chat open and a file preview beside the room, the panel now opens over the room instead of
  squeezing the room's messages to one letter per line.
