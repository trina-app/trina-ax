- Voice input on silence, room noise or a beep now says **Nothing was recognized**. It used to put a stray word such as
  "you", "(beep)" or "Thanks for watching!" into the message box. A short word you really say, like "Yes" or "Thank
  you", is kept. Meeting notes no longer get such lines either, and silence is no longer sent to the speech service.
- Voice input and transcripts of recordings and files no longer start with sound notes such as "[Coughing]", "(music)"
  or "[BLANK_AUDIO]". Only the words you said are kept, and words you said in brackets stay as they are.
