- The AI can change words inside an existing PDF, such as a name, a date or an amount. The old words are removed from the file, not covered, and the new ones are written in the same place, size and colour. Words that are part of a picture (a scanned page) can't be changed, and the AI says so.
- Excel files the AI makes can have link formulas (HYPERLINK) to web pages, email addresses and places in the same workbook. Links to files or network folders are still refused, because opening one could send a coworker's Windows sign-in to another computer.
