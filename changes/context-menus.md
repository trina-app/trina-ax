- A **right-click on a picture or a file** now opens the app's own menu, as in Slack: **Copy image** (pictures),
  **Copy link**, **Download** and **Ask Trina about this file**. It works on photos and files in rooms, DMs, group chats
  and threads, on the picture in the full-screen image viewer, on attachments in an AI chat (copy image, copy link,
  download) and on rows in Files, whose ⋯ menu also gains Copy image and Copy link. The menu key or Shift+F10 opens it
  from the keyboard, a long press on a touch screen, and Shift+right-click keeps the browser's own menu. Copy link copies
  the app's address of the message or file, which only people who can see it can open. In the desktop app, Copy image
  puts the original picture on the clipboard through the app itself.
