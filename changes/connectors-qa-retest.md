- Admin › Connectors: **Connect for the team** is for the whole team unless you choose groups or people under Advanced.
  Before, only you were ticked.
- A connection whose last try failed says when it was last tried, not "Not used yet".
- Admin › Connectors › Google Drive describes what the team gets, not "only you use this connection".
- Connecting a team template with a sign-in now says you sign in on the service's own page and the app keeps the
  sign-in, not your password. The words about a stored key stay for services that use a key.
- A listed service's page (Notion, Linear, Atlassian, Figma, Canva, Stripe, Supabase) shows its privacy, terms and help
  pages before you connect it. Notion, Figma, Stripe and Supabase also show the tools they list on their own pages.
