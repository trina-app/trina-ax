- What you type in a conversation's message box is now kept on your device, like AI chat drafts. Closing the app or
  pressing **Reload** for a new version no longer loses it; the box shows it again when you come back.
- A message that couldn't be sent is still there after you close and reopen the app, marked **Couldn't send** with
  **Try again** and **Discard**. Trying again never posts it twice. Drafts and unsent messages belong to the person
  who wrote them and are removed when they sign out.
