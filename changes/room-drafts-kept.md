- What you type in a conversation's message box is now kept on your device, like AI chat drafts. Closing the app or
  pressing **Reload** for a new version no longer loses it; the box shows it again when you come back.
- A message that hadn't gone out when you closed the app is still there when you open the conversation again, marked
  "Not sure this was sent. Trying again won't send it twice." with **Try again** and **Discard**. Drafts and unsent
  messages belong to the person who wrote them, are shared between that person's tabs, are removed when they sign out
  or leave the conversation, and are kept for 30 days at most.
