- **Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs and Google Sheets** are connectors now, next to Google Drive. Open **Connected apps** in the sidebar, pick one and press **Connect**: Google's sign-in opens at once, and after you confirm the account the AI can use it in chats. Reading is free; sending an email, changing an event, a Doc or a Sheet asks on a card first, and **Always allow** on that card (or **Settings** on the app's page) stops asking for that app. Each app is one sign-in of your own; disconnecting one leaves the others. In a team chat room @Trina can use the asker's own Google apps, and what it finds is shown only to the asker until they share it. Gmail attachments (PDF, HWP, Office files, pictures) are read like Drive files. Linux servers run the open-source Google Workspace MCP server (2.4.1, pinned by hash) on their own machine for this; it keeps nothing, and each call carries the person's own sign-in. Not yet in the Mac app.
- **Connected apps** has its own place in the sidebar: search, what you have connected as a row of icons, and Popular with a + on each. Each connector has its own page with example prompts that start a chat, what the AI can do, information and all its tools; once connected, **Try in chat**, **Settings** and ⋯ (Reconnect, Disconnect). Settings › Connected apps opens the same page. Adding a service by its address is for owners and admins; a member reads "Not listed? Ask an admin to add it."
- Admins set up each Google app in Admin › Connected apps: **Use the same Google app as Google Drive** finishes it in one step, with the list of Google APIs to switch on. Members may connect their own account right away (a switch per app, on by default). Google Drive's members switch is now on by default for new teams; existing teams keep theirs.
- Hosted teams sign in through the hosting service, once it offers these apps. Database migration 341 (add-only).
