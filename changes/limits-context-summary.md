- In a long chat the AI no longer forgets the beginning after an edit, a regenerate, every Codex message or a change of
  person in a shared chat: it gets a short summary of the older part (facts, decisions, files, open tasks) along with
  the recent messages, which now hold about four times as much English text. While the summary is made the answer
  shows "Summary of the earlier part of this chat".
- Chats with pictures no longer start over after a few answers: older pictures are taken out of the saved
  conversation instead, and the AI opens the file again when it needs to look.
