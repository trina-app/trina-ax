- **Hands-on demo build** (`bun run build:demo`): the real web app with a scripted backend that runs entirely in the
  browser, for a try-it-yourself demo on the website. It can be hosted as static files, needs no account, server, AI or
  database, and every visitor gets a fresh copy in their own tab. The story ("Mina's first day", English first) is a
  content file: a welcome, a structured company answer, a toast from the team lead, a banner redone with five
  deliverables and sent to the team room in one click, live replies and reactions, an email with a confirm card and an
  end card with the day's numbers. One guide line shows and does the next step; "Start over" or a reload begins
  again. The normal build is unchanged.
