- **Shared chats show who else is in them in every list.** The Trina list, its history, the phone's list, a project's
  page and search now show the faces of the other people who wrote in a shared chat, in place of the people icon
  (up to 3 faces on a computer, 2 on a phone). Hover the faces, or move to the row with the keyboard, to see their
  names. A shared chat only you have written in keeps the people icon; your private chats look the same as before.
