- **A page that won't open no longer stops at a question.** When Trina can't open a page or find something with its
  own tools and one of your connected services can read it, Trina uses that service right away and says which one. It
  no longer asks "shall I try …?". A connected service that would change something still asks on its card.
- **Taking back a 1:1 message names the person properly.** In Korean the notice now reads "박준호님에게 보낸 글을
  지웠어요" (a room still reads "#영업팀에"), and the sent card says "박준호님에게 보냈어요". Japanese and Chinese use the
  person form too.
