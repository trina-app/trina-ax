- In a shared chat or a team project chat, Trina now does everything it does in a chat only you see: it names the
  chat, moves or renames skills, puts team files in the trash, changes a project's members or who can open a folder
  (each on its usual card), reads your team chat rooms, files teammates shared with you, what you have open, your
  folders, your Google Drive and Slack, and connects a new service. When it would bring in something some people in the
  chat can't open, it asks you first; Google Drive and Slack show the same one-time notice as your other connections.
  Before, Trina said these were not available and sent you to a private chat.
