- Every menu of an AI chat, a conversation and a project now lists its items in the same order: pin, notifications,
  copy link, rename, move, then hide, leave and delete at the end. A project's page menu gains **Copy link** and
  **Rename** (its old "Edit" is now **Project settings**), a conversation's and a project's menu gain **Open in the app**
  on a computer, and a conversation's menu on a phone has **Rename** again.
- Moving a project to the trash now shows **Undo**. Deleting a scheduled task and leaving a group chat now say so in a
  notice. **Leave** is red in every menu.
- One word per action in every language: **Undo** (실행 취소), **Delete** (삭제), **Rename**, **Copy link**, **Hide from my
  list**, **Move to trash** (휴지통으로 옮기기), **Don't allow** (허용 안 함). In Korean an AI chat is always 채팅.
