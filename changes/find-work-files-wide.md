- **Find work files on this computer** now also looks in your cloud drives' folders (OneDrive, Google Drive, Dropbox,
  Box), work folders named like your company or a client, messenger downloads (KakaoTalk, Telegram, WhatsApp …), and
  the files you opened lately anywhere in your home folder (on Windows also other drives and network drives). Each file
  says where it is. Files that are only in the cloud are not downloaded until you add them; mail, messages, photos and
  other apps' data are never looked at.
- On a Mac, the app first asks for **Full Disk Access** (one switch in System Settings) instead of a question per
  folder; **Not now** keeps the three folders with macOS's questions as before.
