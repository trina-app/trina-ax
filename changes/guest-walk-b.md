- Admin Home has a **Guests** card ("1 of 3 guests" and their names) that opens Members with only the guests listed.
  The Members card now counts team members only.
- Admin › Members › Guests: a guest who left on their own reads **Left '…' on their own**, one taken out reads
  **Removed**, and the button says which it shows ("Show guests who left or were removed"). A guest whose project is in
  the trash stays in the list with **Restore project**. The title's count reads "1 of 3", without "guests" a second time.
- Admin's **Invite people** dialog is titled **Invite a team member** or **Invite a guest**, by the tab picked.
- A group chat is called a **group chat** everywhere (its menu, info, leave, rename, the guest lines, pickers and
  search); a guest is always a **guest**, and someone made a member is a **team member**. In Korean the owner is 팀장
  everywhere, and a group chat's manager is 대화 관리자 / **Chat manager**. Adding people to a group chat says **Add to
  group chat**.
- A guest's leave dialog no longer says their account is deleted: they can't sign in any more until invited again.
- Korean texts no longer show "을(를)" after a name the app can't read aloud, such as a project called "Budget Review".
