- Files: the folder panel is now **Who can open it**, laid out like Google Drive's sharing window. Admins see the people and groups who can open the folder, each with **Can view** or **Can edit**, and one **General access** setting (only people added here, everyone in the team, or the same as the folder above). They change it right there: add people or groups, change or remove them, and save. Before saving, one line names who will gain or lose access, and giving more people access asks first. People and groups that a folder above lets in show greyed, with a way to change them in that folder. The list of everyone who cannot open the folder, the reasons under each name and the counts line are gone. A team project's folder is shown, not changed, here: its members are changed in the project. Members see what they saw before.
- Admin › Folder permissions: the Save button and the summary of a change keep its outcomes apart: "can no longer open it", "can no longer edit", "can now open it" and "can now edit". Someone who goes from read & write to read was counted as losing access; they can still open the folder.
