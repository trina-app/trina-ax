- The AI names team skills by the name people see in the app (for example "견적서 작성"), not by their folder name
  ("quote-writer"). Asking for a skill by that name also finds it.
- When a member asks the AI to change a team skill they can't edit, it offers the **Suggest as skill** button or a
  personal copy. It no longer pastes the whole changed skill for them to copy by hand.
- Asked about a project they are not in, a person is now told that they are not a member and who can add them. The
  AI still doesn't say whether that project exists.
- In a project's shared chat, a member who can't change who is in the project is told who can (the person who made it
  or an admin). They are no longer sent to a private chat that would refuse them too.
- The AI no longer talks about "the server" when it explains what it can and can't do.
