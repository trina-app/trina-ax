- Trina now remembers lasting things on its own, little by little, as Claude Code does: which account or profile works
  for what, your preferences and corrections ("always tables first", "don't attach, send a link"), and stable facts
  about your work. It never asks with a card; the answer shows a small "Remembered: …" step with **Undo**, and its words
  open Settings › Memory. Changing or forgetting a memory shows the same step. The first time, one line says how to see
  or turn this off.
- Settings › Memory has a new switch, **Remember automatically** (on). Off, Trina remembers only what you ask it to.
- Trina never keeps passwords, keys or codes, words copied from mail, web pages, files or other tool output, or an
  address that only such content showed. It does not remember on its own in shared chats, rooms, scheduled runs or
  confidential chats. Your own memories come first in what Trina reads.
