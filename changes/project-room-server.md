- A team project now has its own chat room. It is made the first time someone opens it, is named after the project,
  and its people are the project's people: adding someone to the project adds them to the room, and removing someone
  takes them out. People who join later see the room's history. While the project is in the trash its room is hidden;
  restoring the project brings it back.
- People who can only view a project can write in its chat room but cannot attach files from their computer.
- Admin notes: the "private channel linked to a project" of 0.5.21 is retired. At the first start after the update, a
  linked channel whose people are exactly the project's people becomes the project's chat room (one line in it says
  so; nobody sees older messages than before). Any other linked channel loses its link and stays an ordinary private
  channel with the same people and messages. The old link, unlink, "Create a channel" and Undo of "Save to project"
  calls now answer 410.
- Admin notes, going back to an older version: project rooms then show as private channels linked to their project,
  with the old link options, the 100-person limit and the older guest checks; a member added or removed there by hand
  is put back at the next update.
