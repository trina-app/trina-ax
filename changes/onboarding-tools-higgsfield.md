- Getting started, **Tools for your team**: the rows are now Notion, Higgsfield (AI images and video), Google Drive and
  Gmail. Higgsfield works like Notion: **Connect for the team** signs in one Higgsfield account for everyone, or
  **Ask everyone to connect their own**. Each team row now also says what the tool does. Slack is no longer a row,
  because it takes more steps to set up. It is under **Other tools**, where an owner or admin whose team has not added
  Slack gets a Slack card that opens **Admin › Connected apps › Slack**.
