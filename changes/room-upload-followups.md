- A large file you are sending in a chat keeps going up when you open another chat or another page. The chat's row
  shows a small "Uploading a file" mark meanwhile, and the file waits in that chat's message box until you press send
  there. Closing or reloading the tab while a file is going up asks first.
- Dropping many large files at once works: they go up three at a time and the others say "Waiting for its turn". Before,
  the eleventh file could fail after a few seconds.
- A large upload says what is happening instead of only spinning: "Checking the file", its percent, "Finishing".
- An upload that never started (a tab closed right away, a file taken out at once) no longer holds storage room for a
  day: it is cleared after an hour.
- A guest can upload files of up to 100 MB (in a chat, in their project's folder, in an AI chat). The owner or an admin can raise that to 1 GB or 10 GB in Admin >
  Members & invites, above the Guests list. Team members can send up to 10 GB as before.
