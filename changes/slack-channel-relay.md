- **Connect a Slack channel to a conversation.** In a team conversation or a project, an owner or admin picks **Add an
  app › Slack**, chooses a channel they and the Trina AX app are in, and presses **Connect**. From then on everyone in
  the conversation sees the channel's messages, with the sender's Slack name and picture, and what they write goes to
  the channel as "Name (Trina)". Threads, mentions, edits and deletes follow both ways. AI answers and files are not
  sent to Slack; such a message says "Not sent to Slack". Works with Slack Connect channels shared with a client.
