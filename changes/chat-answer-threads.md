- **Threads on AI answers in shared chats.** In a chat shared with a project or with people, press **Reply in thread**
  under an answer to talk it over with your teammates beside the chat. Trina doesn't read these replies. When you've
  settled it, **Send to Trina** puts the thread in the chat's box as a quote, so you can edit it and send it as your own
  question. The person who asked and everyone who replied are notified (Settings › Notifications › "Replies in threads
  I'm part of").
