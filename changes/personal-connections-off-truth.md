- When a team turns off **Members can add their own connections**, a member's own connection now says **Off** in
  Settings › Connectors and on its page, with a line that it is not used in chats, and it can no longer be signed in
  again until the switch is back on. The AI no longer says such a connection "is ready in this chat"; it says the team
  turned members' own connections off and to ask an admin.
- The switch's hint in Team settings now says that connections set up from a team template and Google apps such as
  Gmail and Drive keep working while it is off.
- A connector's sign-in window allowed after more than 10 minutes now says the sign-in expired and to connect again from
  the app, instead of asking you to sign in to the team account.
