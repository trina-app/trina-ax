- Answers in shared project chats and team chat rooms are no longer hidden from people who get access later. The read card now says that what the AI finds stays in the chat for everyone who sees it, including people who get access later, and sending an answer to a team chat room says that people who join later will see it too. Older answers that no card covered stay hidden as before.
- Adding people to a project now warns when they would start seeing answers that used places they can't open, and **Show answers** lists those answers. Each one opens in the chat at that answer; on a phone, **Back to sharing** returns to the list with your picks.
