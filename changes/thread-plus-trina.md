- In a conversation, a thread and the **✦** side chat now stay open together: the conversation, the thread and the side
  chat as three columns. Opening ✦ used to close the thread. On a window too small for three, the list of conversations
  folds away first; smaller still, the thread and the side chat share the window and the conversation waits under
  them; on a phone the side chat opens over the thread and ‹ goes back to it.
- With a thread open, the side chat's box says **Looking at this thread**: your question is about that thread, its
  answer offers **Send to thread** first, and an empty chat offers **Summarize this thread**. Press ✕ on that line to
  ask about the whole conversation again. The AI reads the thread as you may see it.
- A thread open under the side chat, or a conversation under the two, is no longer marked read while you cannot see it.
- Meeting notes: the small card no longer sits on the message box of a thread or side chat on the right.
