- **Slack** is no longer an Admin menu item of its own. The owner finds it in **Admin › Connected apps**, like Google
  Drive: under **Connected** once it is added (with On, Off, a problem or Reconnect on its row), else under
  **Discover**. Its page has the same Add to Slack, steps, status, Turn off and Remove as before. Old links to Admin ›
  Slack, and the way back from Slack after **Add to Slack**, open it there.
- **Slack**: after the app was removed in Slack, its page also offers **Remove Slack**, so it can be taken out of the
  list instead of waiting there to be reconnected.
- Docs site: the Slack guide's screenshots are now of the English app in the light theme, showing Slack in Connected
  apps.
