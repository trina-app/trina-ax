- Trina can unzip a .zip file in the team folder: "unzip it and leave it there". The files go into a new folder next to
  the zip, named after it, with their folders and names kept (Korean names too, also from zips made on Windows).
  Nothing is saved over an existing file, and Trina tells you what was saved and anything that wasn't. Password-protected,
  damaged or very large zips, and zips with unsafe paths, are refused; Trina can also just list what is inside.
