- A question the AI asks you on a card now waits for as long as you need. Before, the card closed after 10 minutes and
  the AI went on without your answer. Now the answer ends while the card stays open, also after a reload or a server
  restart, and your answer, even hours later, continues the same conversation.
- On longer jobs the AI can keep a to-do list: one checklist card under its answer that shows what is not started, in
  progress and done, updated as it works and kept with the answer.
