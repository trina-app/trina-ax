- Trying connectors no longer blocks adding one. Each **Connect** saves the connection before its sign-in, so after 20
  tries at services that refuse the app (Canva, Figma, Intercom, Square) or sign-in windows you closed, the next
  Connect said "You can have at most 20 connections" while nothing was connected. Now a new Connect replaces your
  oldest connection that never signed in. A connection with a sign-in or key, one made from a template your admin set
  up, one with your own app, or one you tried in the last hour is never replaced. The limit message now says "working connections", the
  connections it counts.
