- **Team export as a normal ZIP**: on the hosted service, the owner's export now downloads as a ZIP that opens with a double-click on Mac and Windows: the team's folders (personal folders by name), every channel, direct message and AI chat as text files and as JSON, and a README.txt that says what is where. No passphrase or terminal is needed. The encrypted file for restoring stays behind a small "Encrypted backup" link; its passphrase is now eight groups of five letters and digits with nothing to mix up (no 0/O or 1/l).
- Self-hosted: `trina_admin archive --zip --out team.zip` writes the same ZIP.
