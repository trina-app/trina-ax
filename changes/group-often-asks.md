- The new chat screen no longer shows the **Frequently used skills** card or **My frequent questions**. In their place,
  under the box and without a frame: what people in your group often ask (or your team, if your groups have no list),
  three short questions with **Show more** for up to 10. A click puts the question in the box; it never sends. Each one
  is a general sentence the AI writes once a day from what 3 or more people of that group or team asked in a similar
  way, with no names, numbers or file names, and nobody can see who asked or how often. Only questions people type in
  their own AI chats count (never project chats, rooms, side chats, Slack or scheduled tasks), and only from this
  update on. A group or team shows a list once 5 or more of its people asked something. It uses the team's AI account once a day, never anyone's own.
  Admins can turn it off in Admin › Team settings › Members and AI ("Show what the team often asks", on by default).
- For newcomers, the team skills in **Ready in your team** now step aside after 5 chats even if no team skill was run.
