- Connecting an app from the desktop app no longer asks you to sign in again in the browser. After you sign in at the
  service, the browser hands the connection back to the app and it finishes there. This covers MCP apps, GitHub and
  apps with your own Client ID. A link that has expired now says so plainly: go back to the app and press Connect again.
- The page the browser shows after signing in to an app now looks like the rest of Trina AX: the logo, light and dark,
  readable on a phone, with one clear next step.
