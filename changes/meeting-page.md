- **Meeting notes in the Mac app.** My files has **Take meeting notes**. After a yes in the app's own dialog, the app
  listens to your microphone and the sound of your Zoom, Meet or Teams call, and writes down what is said as you talk
  ("You" and "Others"). A small card shows the words, the time and **Stop**, and the Mac menu bar shows a red dot while
  it listens. Stop saves the note to My files › Meetings. The sound is turned into text by your team's speech service
  and is not kept. Needs macOS 14.4 or later, and voice input turned on for the team.
