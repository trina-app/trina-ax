- **The AI can now use your own browser.** Install the extension in Chrome, Edge or Brave, open your team in that
  browser and click its icon, then Connect. The AI then opens sites and acts there with the accounts you are signed in
  to, from the web app, the desktop app (Settings › Browser › Use my Chrome) and your scheduled tasks. It works for
  hosted teams too.
- Connect each browser profile you keep for an account (work mail, personal …) and give it a name: the AI picks the
  right profile for each step by itself, and can switch between them within one answer.
- Each task gets its own background window in that profile, so several chats can use the browser at once without
  touching the tabs you are working in. Stop one from the extension's icon.
- The AI asks once per site and profile ("Always allow this site" holds from then on, also for scheduled tasks). Your
  team's own pages and account-security pages are never opened for it. Settings › Browser lists your browsers with
  their allowed sites; disconnect one any time.
