- **A welcome for people you invite.** Someone who joins with a team link, a single-use invite or **Add directly** now
  sees one card at the top of Home: "Welcome to {team}", who invited them and how many people are here, and four small
  steps: the conversations they are in, **👋 Hi** (one tap says hello in Everyone), a profile photo (optional) and **Ask
  Trina what you can do here** (one tap; Trina lists the team skills, folders and conversations they can use, in plain
  words). Without an AI the last step is how to get one (connect their own, or tell the owner). It follows them across
  devices and goes away when done or hidden.
- Someone an admin adds with **Add directly** is now announced like an invite or team link join, when they first sign
  in: "{name} joined the team" in Everyone, and the owners see it in Activity (not the owner who added them).
- The join page says who invited you ("Alex invited you to Acme"), for team links and single-use invites.
- A new member's first Activity item is no longer a connector to set up: those wait until they open Connected apps, where
  they are listed.
- Rooms say once what ✦ does: "✦ Ask Trina about this room. Only you see it." Close it or use ✦ and it never comes back.
- Opening a team link while already signed in says "You're already in {team}". Files no longer calls **Personal**
  read-only (your own folder is inside), and a greyed New folder / Upload says why.
