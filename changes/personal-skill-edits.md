- When you correct how Trina did a task, it now updates every place of its own skill that the correction affects.
  Before, a change that kept most of a line's words was refused, so a skill could end up saying two different things.
- Skills Trina saves for you have a name in your language (for example "주간보고"). The line under the answer and
  the approval card use that name, not a file name.
- A step whose approval card is still open now shows as waiting, and as done or declined once you answer. Before,
  it showed a check mark straight away.
