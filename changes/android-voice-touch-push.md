  that Android needs before it can use the microphone, so it said "Microphone access is blocked" even after you
  allowed the microphone.

  really refused. If you closed the browser's question, it asks you to tap the mic again. If no microphone was found,
  or it couldn't start, it says that instead.

  the first line yet. They still say only "You have a new message"; the setting applies to your computer and iPhone.
