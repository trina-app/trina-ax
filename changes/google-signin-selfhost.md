- A server you run yourself with a public web address can offer **Sign in with Google** through Trina AX's sign-in
  service, with no Google Cloud project to make: the server registers itself, as it does for Google Drive. Trina turns
  the service on for self-installed servers after testing.
- New page **Admin › Sign-in methods**: the password, and Sign in with Google with its switch, a line that says how it
  signs in here (or that the server needs a public web address), and **Try it**, which opens Google and shows which
  account answered without signing anyone in. The Google row moved here from Team settings, without "Set up on the
  Google Drive page".
- Optional: **Only Google Workspace accounts from** lets in only Google accounts of your company's Workspace domains,
  for signing in, connecting and joining. Trina AX asks Google which Workspace an account belongs to; it never goes by
  the e-mail address. Off by default, so personal Gmail and teams without Workspace keep working.
- When Trina AX's sign-in service can't be reached, people who have no password still see the Google button, and a
  click says to try again in a few minutes or ask an admin for a temporary password.
