- Teams hosted by Trina that sign in with Google through Trina's sign-in service now see the **Sign in with Google**
  switch in **Admin › Team settings**. It said "This needs a Google app" there.
- The Google buttons use Google's own words and mark: **Sign in with Google**, **Sign up with Google** on the setup page
  and **Continue with Google** on invites and the team link, with Google's colour "G". They come first, above "or" and
  the password form.
- Joining with Google from an invite or the team link no longer asks for a name and a username first: the name comes
  from Google and the username from the Google address. Both can be changed later in Profile. Guest invites still use a
  password.
