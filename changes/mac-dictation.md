- On a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26, voice input in the desktop app is now written by the Mac itself: your words
  show in the box about a second after you start talking and keep up while you talk, and the last word is there a
  moment after you stop. The sound never leaves your Mac, so this also works in confidential chats. The first time,
  the Mac fetches its speech model for your language (the bar shows how far); other computers and languages work as
  before.
- In the Mac app, a microphone that macOS blocked now says where to turn it on (System Settings › Privacy & Security ›
  Microphone), with an Open Settings link.
