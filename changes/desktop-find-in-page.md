- Desktop app: **Cmd+F** (Ctrl+F on Windows and Linux), or Edit › Find…, opens a find bar on the screen in front: the
  team page or the in-app browser. Selected text fills it, matches are highlighted with "2 of 5", Enter or Cmd+G goes to
  the next one, Shift+Enter or Shift+Cmd+G to the one before, and Esc closes it. A site with its own Cmd+F keeps it.
