- A member who opens Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs or Google Sheets before the team has set it up
  can tap **Ask admin to add it** on its page. The owner and the admins get one notice in Activity ("Alice and 2 others
  asked to add Gmail") that opens the app's setup, and once it is added each member who asked gets a quiet notice that
  it is ready. A request is sent once; asking again says "Already asked". Members never see who the admins are.
