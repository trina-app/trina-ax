- Trina can make photos smaller: "make these photos smaller", "1200 px wide", "under 2MB" or "for the web". It saves a
  new copy next to each photo (JPEG, PNG or WebP, up to 20 at a time) and leaves the original as it was. Phone photos
  stay upright, the location is removed from the copies, and Trina tells you each new size. iPhone HEIC photos can't
  be resized yet; Trina says so and how to save them as JPEG.
