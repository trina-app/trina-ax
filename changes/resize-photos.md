- Trina can make photos smaller: "make these photos smaller", "1200 px wide", "under 2MB" or "for the web". It saves a
  new copy next to each photo (JPEG, PNG, WebP or iPhone HEIC, up to 20 at a time) and leaves the original as it was.
  Phone photos stay upright, and Trina tells you each new size. The copies keep the photo's details (when and where it
  was taken, the camera); say "without the location" to leave the location out. iPhone photos come out as JPEG.
- Trina can look at iPhone photos (HEIC) you share or keep in the team folder. A server that can't open them says so
  in one sentence and asks for a JPEG.
