# Example Coffee company intro

> Fictional data. Example Coffee is a company made up for Trina's skill examples. Every name, person, number and
> address in it is invented.

## In one line
Example Coffee is a 12-person coffee roaster that sends beans roasted every week to offices and homes.

## What we do
- **Online shop**: 6 single-origin coffees, 2 blends and drip bags.
- **Office coffee subscription**: we size the order to the team and deliver every week or every other week. Grinders
  and drippers are on loan.
- **Roasting**: we roast every Tuesday and Friday and ship within 48 hours of roasting.

## Who it is for
- Offices of 10 to 200 people: the office manager who wants better coffee in the kitchen
- People who brew at home and want small, frequent deliveries

## What our customers struggle with
- Nobody owns the office coffee order, so it runs out.
- Beans bought in bulk go stale quickly.

## Numbers (as of October 2026)
- 120 offices on the subscription
- Shipped within 48 hours of roasting
- 92% of office subscriptions renewed in 2025

## How we work
1. We ask how many people drink coffee and how much, and size the subscription.
2. We roast on Tuesday and Friday.
3. We ship within 48 hours of roasting.
4. Once a month we ask what is left and adjust the next month.

## What a customer said
"The beans arrive just as we run low. Nobody has to remember the kitchen order any more." (office subscriber, a
30-person company)
