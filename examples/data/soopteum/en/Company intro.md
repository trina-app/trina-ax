# Soopteum company intro

> Fictional data. Soopteum is a company made up for the Trina company-profile example. Its name, people, numbers,
> address and phone number are all invented.

## In one line
Soopteum picks, installs and keeps office plants alive on one subscription. Plant subscriptions for offices, from
setup to fortnightly care.

## Basics
- Founded: 2019
- Based in: Seongsu, Seoul
- Team: 18 people
- Area: Seoul & Gyeonggi

## What customers struggle with
- Nobody waters them: when the office manager changes, plants are forgotten.
- Dead pots pile up: clearing them falls to the admin team.
- The wrong species: light and air conditioning decide what lives.

## What we do
One contract, and the plants are our job.
- Space check: we measure light, heat and foot traffic.
- Installation: plants, pots and stands in one visit.
- Fortnightly care: water, pruning and leaf cleaning.
- Replacement: a plant that fails is replaced free.

## Numbers (as of October 2026)
- 340 client offices
- 12,000 plants in our care
- 96% renewal rate

## How we work
Site visit, proposal, install, then care, in that order. Two weeks from first call to first visit.
1. Site visit: we measure and photograph the space.
2. Proposal: a plan and a quote.
3. Install: done in a day.
4. Care: same weekday, every other week.
