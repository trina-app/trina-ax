# A Slack-style status: an emoji, a short text and a time it clears itself

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-10 01:53
- Branch: `feat/custom-status`
- Status: in progress
- Where: trina-ax core (`server/src/auth/status-messages.ts`, `server/src/routes/people.ts`, `server/migrations/`,
  `shared/src/api/people.ts`, `server/src/rooms/reader.ts`, `server/src/connectors/remote-mcp/groups.ts`,
  `web/src/components/StatusMessage.tsx` and the screens that show names). Why: people, profiles and team chat are
  core (see docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md)
- Version impact: minor. A visible feature, two endpoints and one additive table; older apps ignore the new field.

## Goal

The owner showed Slack's "상태 설정" dialog and wants ours like it: a person says "🌴 휴가 중 · 11/14까지" and the
team sees it next to their name, without asking. Today there is only the online dot (docs/DECISIONS.md "접속 상태");
the owner's earlier "한 줄 상태는 쓰지 않는다" is replaced by this decision (the old unused `status_text` stays unused).

## Scope

In:
- **Dialog "상태 설정"** (one `Modal`: a bottom sheet on phones), opened from the account menu next to 자리 비움, by
  clicking your own status in the sidebar, and from a row in the phone's drawer.
  - An input "상태가 어떻게 되나요?" (100 characters) with an emoji button. The app has no emoji picker (reactions are
    a fixed strip of 8), so the button opens a small fixed grid of status emoji in the reactions' style.
  - Empty input: five suggestions, "emoji **name** — when it clears": 📅 회의 중 — 1시간, 🚌 출퇴근 중 — 30분, 🤒 병가 —
    오늘, 🌴 휴가 중 — 날짜 정하기, 🏡 재택 근무 — 오늘. A pick fills the input; typing your own is fine.
  - Filled input: "삭제 시간": 지우지 않음, 30분, 1시간, 4시간, 오늘, 이번 주, 날짜 정하기. 날짜 정하기 shows a start and an
    end date; a later start shows the status only from that day. Editing a set status offers "그대로" (its own end).
  - 취소 and 저장; with a status set, "상태 지우기".
- **Server**: table `user_status_messages` (migration 283: 281 and 282 are taken by open PRs #275 and #284), one row
  per person: emoji, text, start, clear time, last day, the choice. `PUT /api/me/status`, `DELETE /api/me/status`,
  own only. The server works out every end in the team's time zone (`teamTimeZone`, `zonedParts`, `zonedToUtc`), never
  its own: 오늘 ends at the team's midnight, 이번 주 at the end of Sunday, dates at the end of the last day.
  - Validation: one emoji (a single emoji or one emoji sequence: ZWJ, flag, keycap, skin tone), shared by web and
    server; text trimmed, at most 100, no control, format or direction characters (only the emoji joiner); dates real,
    end not before start, at most 366 days ahead. Text only: React text everywhere, attribute-safe for the AI.
  - Expiry: hidden on read at once (start and end compared with now), removed by a timer that runs at the next start or
    end (at least hourly) and on every write. A start or an end tells open windows.
  - Audience: exactly the presence audience: `GET /api/people` (signed-in, active, not in a forced password change),
    and only active accounts' statuses are given (a disabled or deleted account's never; deletion removes the row).
  - Live: the existing `profile_changed` / `my_profile_changed` notices (the people list and my account are read
    again), at a write and when the timer passes a start or an end. A short per-person write limit keeps one person
    from making every window of the team read the list again and again.
  - `Person.statusMessage` (the team's view: only a live status) and `Me.user.statusMessage` (my own, also one that
    starts later); `docs:api`.
- **Where it shows** (an emoji after the name; the text and "~까지" on hover, and on a tap where the emoji is a button
  of its own): the sidebar's DM and unnamed group-chat rows and the phone's conversation list, the member list, the
  message author line in rooms and threads, the @ list, my own account row (a click opens the dialog), the phone's
  drawer (a row with my status). In a DM with someone who has a status: one quiet line above the box, "🌴 민지 님: 휴가
  중 · 11/14까지". There is no profile card or popover in the app yet; the member list is its place.
- **Trina knows it**: `room_read`'s member line (the in-app AI and AI apps) names each member's status when set, also
  for members beyond the names it lists, and `people_list` (AI apps, admins) gives it: "status: 🌴 휴가 중 until
  2026-11-14". Same rule: active accounts, live statuses only. No new tool.
- DECISIONS entry "상태 메시지", one CHANGELOG line, every new text in ko, en, id, ja, es.

Out (later):
- Slack's "자동으로 업데이트" from Google Calendar.
- Suggestions an admin can edit.
- A status on message avatars or a profile card (no card exists), a status history, a status per device.

## Risks

- Audience: a status must never reach anyone the online dot does not: the directory route is the only read path, and
  the AI's lines use the same visibility rule. Deep review tries to read a status from outside and to inject markup.
- Text in other people's screens and in the AI's context: plain text only, control and direction characters refused,
  quotes and tags neutralised in the AI's lines.
- Time zones: the end of 오늘 / 이번 주 is the team's, tested across zones.
- Migration 283 is additive (reader 1). Five languages; phones (sheet, drawer, tap); the shared name components.
