- Version impact: patch (wrong count and clearer words; no API shape, schema or protocol change).

## Goal

The claude.ai parity pass over chat sharing (AP-045..061) found three things that mislead people:

1. In a team project with a guest, a shared project chat says "3 people can see this chat" (and "Link copied · 3
   people can open it") while the guest cannot open it: other members' project chats stay hidden from guests
   (docs/DECISIONS.md "Guests: only what they were invited to"). `Chat.shared.audience` used the project's audience,
   not the chat's (`Audiences.ofChat`, which leaves guests out but the chat's own creator).
2. A guest who opens a teammate's chat link reads "Ask the person who sent the link to share it with you" and an
   "Ask Trina" link, although nobody can share a member's chat with a guest. The guest gets their own words.
3. The Share dialog says people "can ask the AI in it themselves" but not whose AI usage that is. Each person's
   question runs on their own AI connection and usage limit ("A turn is its sender's"); the note says so.

## Scope

In: the count, the guest's not-found text, the share note (six languages), unit tests.
Out: the project box's "N people" chip still counts the project's audience (guests included); `Project.audience` is
also the file-access count, where guests do count, so changing it needs its own field.

## Steps

1. `sharedOf` (project kind): `audience: aud.ofChat(r).length`. Unit test with a guest member.
2. Chat page not-found fallback: a guest gets `chat.notFoundGuest` and a link to Projects.
3. `chat.share.note` / `noteShort`: "each on their own AI usage". `bun run check:i18n`.

## Decisions

None new: all three follow recorded decisions (guests, a turn is its sender's).
