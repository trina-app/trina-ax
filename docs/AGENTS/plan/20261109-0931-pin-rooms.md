- Version impact: minor, a new endpoint pair, an additive field and an additive migration.

## Goal

The owner, after #245 (pinned AI chats): the same for the team chat list. A room, DM or group chat in the sidebar's
**대화** and on /rooms gets **상단에 고정** / **고정 해제** in its "⋯", and pinned conversations stay at the top, for
the person who pinned them only.

## Scope

In:
- Migration 278 (B): `room_pins(user_id, room_id, pinned_at)`, one row per person and room, cascading with the room and
  the person.
- `POST` / `DELETE /api/rooms/:id/pin`, members only (404 otherwise), idempotent, answer the room. `Room.pinnedAt` for
  the viewer wherever a room is given (list, one room, `room.updated`). At most 50 pins (409 `room_pins_full`).
- Clean-up: a pin goes when its person leaves or is taken out (every way out runs `forgetMember`), when the room is
  deleted (cascade; archived rooms have no members), when the person is deleted (their DM rows stay as tombstones),
  and so when they can no longer see it (in a room = a member of it).
- Hidden rooms: **hiding a room unpins it**. A hidden room's "⋯" has no 상단에 고정 (it has 다시 보이기), and the
  server refuses to pin one (409 `room_hidden`), so a room is never pinned and hidden at once.
- A pin or unpin tells only my own windows (`room.updated` to me).
- Web: the shared room menu's first item (rows of the sidebar and /rooms: hover, right-click, long press, the phone
  sheet; and the room header's "⋯", the same component), a small pin after the name on pinned rows, pinned first in
  the sidebar (at most 10 above the five latest) and all of them first on /rooms and the phone's 대화 tab, below the
  트리나 row. Five languages, the chat pin strings reused.

Out: drag to reorder, a "Pinned" header, pinning a hidden room, a pin limit in the UI beyond the server's cap. The
server's list order stays by activity (the app sorts; the list is not paged).
