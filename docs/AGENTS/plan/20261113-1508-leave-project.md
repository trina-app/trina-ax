## Goal

A member of a team project can take themselves out of it ("Leave project"), also when they came in through a group,
without anyone else in that group losing it. Today only the creator or an admin can take someone out.

## Scope

In:
- `POST /api/projects/:id/leave`, for someone who reads a team project in `projects/<name>` (in place, not in the trash).
  - Leave = take out my own by-name rule on the project folder. If I still read it after that (a group, a role or an
    everyone rule), a personal `none` rule for me is added: the person tier decides before the group tier
    (`policy/access.ts`), so the group stays and everyone else in it keeps the project.
  - The creator may leave too. `projects.created_by` is set to NULL then, so the save path stops putting their rule
    back (`participantRules`) and they lose the creator's powers here; admins still manage it.
  - The last person who reads it cannot leave (409 `project_last_member`); the owner and admins who manage it without
    being members do not count. Personal projects: 409 `project_personal`.
  - Effects through the paths a removal by an admin uses: the rules saved with `replaceRules`, the `folder.rules` audit
    (via `leave`, `self`), `afterAccessChange` (sessions, notices, shared chats dropped from their lists). Scheduled
    tasks in the project pause at their next run as after a removal (`schedules.runDue`, `schedule.project_gone`).
    Their private chats in the project move out to their own chats (`project_id = NULL`), so they keep them; their
    shared chats stay for the others. One more audit row `project.leave`.
- The exceptions on the members screen: a group participant carries `except` (the people in it with a `none` rule of
  their own), shown as "Marketing · except Kim". Adding that person by name replaces the exception with write. Taking
  the group out drops the exceptions it leaves without a reason (only when dropping them lets nobody in). Exceptions
  are part of the project, not "other read-only rules".
- `Project.leave`: `'yes'` (shown) or `'last'` (shown, off, with the reason). Absent: a personal project, not a
  member, in the trash.
- Web: "Leave project" in the project page's name menu and in the card's "…" menu; one confirm ("Leave <project>? You
  won't see its files and shared chats anymore. Your private chats in it move to your own chats."). Five languages.

Out:
- Rules on folders inside the project that let the person in by name or by group (an admin's own setting there): not
  changed. Leaving is about the project folder.
- Telling the others someone left.

## Risks

- No migration (created_by is nullable; a deleted account already leaves it NULL).
- A `none` rule only narrows. Dropping an exception is checked: kept when the person would read without it.
- Hunks in `routes/chats.ts` and `Projects.tsx` kept small (another branch works on chat sharing there).
