## Goal

A walk through a real build of onboarding v6 found things a member or an owner trips on before the beta:

- After "팀에 준비된 것" fills the message box (자연스러운 글's 써 보기, 물어보기, the ready question) the caret sits at the
  start, so text pasted next lands before "/humanizer …" and breaks the command.
- Member- and owner-facing copy shows developer paths and English: "groups/예시커피 폴더", "(브랜드, skills 폴더는 빼고)",
  "users/member2 폴더에 …", "skills/ 폴더".
- An empty team's goal card says "팀 스킬은 아직 써 보기 전이에요" with a filled [다시 보내기] although there is no skill.
- Korean copy names the product two ways ("Trina AX가 준비한 스킬" beside "트리나에게 묻기").
- Smaller: the brand line names website photos without a website; phone skill rows hide 써 보기; "스킬 넣기" lands on a
  folded catalog; the member's sheet is "내 스킬 올리기" behind "내 스킬 가져오기"; the box sends "/humanizer …:" alone.

## Scope

In:
- Composer: a programmatic fill (a chosen preset, `prepareDraft`) puts the caret at the end when it focuses the box.
- Composer `waitsFor`: while the box holds only the 자연스러운 글 request, send is off and a quiet line says
  "다듬을 글을 붙여 넣어 주세요".
- 물어보기: the folder by its name ("예시커피 폴더"), the left-out folders read as words ("브랜드 폴더와 스킬 폴더는 빼고").
- The ready question without a profile: a general first question that needs no team file (what the AI can do for this
  team), in five languages; `home.example1` goes.
- The member's empty 팀 스킬 text without "skills/ 폴더".
- Goal card: with no team skill, the line says skills come first and the filled button is [스킬 넣기] (the catalog
  open); [다시 보내기] or the invite stays, quiet.
- `{product}` in the catalog title, the skill sources and the invite message: the name the UI gives its AI in each
  language (`aiName`, "트리나"), never a literal.
- The brand line names website photos only with a website; phone skill rows show 써 보기; `/skills?catalog=open`
  opens the catalog; the member's sheet is titled "내 스킬 가져오기".

Out:
- The AI's own wording, the admin-only skill rows' "general ·" and "/card-news", the MCP dialog's labels.
