# A press on a grouped card approves only what was on screen long enough to be seen

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-23 15:37
- Branch: `fix/016-gate-lock`
- Status: done
- Where: web/src/lib/cardBatch.ts, web/src/components/chat/AskCard.tsx. Why: review of #475, one press could approve a card that joined at the moment of the click
- Version impact: patch, a safety fix to the grouped card of #475

## Goal

A card that joins a group never gets approved by a press aimed at the cards already there.

## Scope

In: a short lock when any card joins, and a press that covers only cards on screen for the lock's length.
Out: the server's "Always allow", which by its meaning covers the kind from then on.

## Steps

1. Pure rule in cardBatch.ts (joinLock), with a cap so repeated arrivals cannot lock the buttons for good.
2. BatchCard uses it for its buttons and for what a press sends.
3. Tests: mixed group, arrival during the click, repeated arrivals.
