## Goal

"Turn this form into a skill" gives one save card that says what is saved in plain words, with every file's content
on the card (folded), and nothing saved that the person did not see.

## Scope

In: a new skill's plain text files (a form or template: .md .txt .csv .tsv, not scripts, not references/, not
instruction-named, not a skill header) written before its SKILL.md in the same answer are held, not saved; the SKILL.md
card lists them (name, size, first lines, full text folded); Save approves exactly those bytes; the AI writes them
again unchanged and they save with no card. Remove the earlier "after SKILL.md, the rest goes without a card" path.
Out: binary templates (make_file, edit_hwp) keep their own card; a multi-file save tool.
