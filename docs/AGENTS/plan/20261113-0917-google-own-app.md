# The Google apps can use the company's own Google app, set once in Team settings

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-13 09:17
- Branch: `feat/google-own-app`
- Status: draft
- Where: trina-ax core (`server/src/integrations/google-drive/`, `server/src/integrations/google-apps/`,
  `web/src/components/integrations/` Google pages, `docs/site/`). Why: Google sign-in and its admin pages are core; no
  control plane change.
- Version impact: minor. A new admin setting with new endpoints and add-only fields; nothing changes for a team that
  does not use it.

## Goal

Trina's own Google app is waiting for Google's review of its Gmail and Drive permissions (about 4 to 6 weeks). Until
then people see Google's "unverified app" screen, and at most 100 people can sign in, counted across every team.

A company on Google Workspace can skip that wait. Its IT admin makes one Google app set to **Internal** (only the
company's own accounts can use it). Google does not review Internal apps, and there is no user limit. The admin adds it
to Trina **once**. From then on Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs and Google Sheets all sign in with
it: members' own connections, Drives shared with the team and the shared team account (#685). People see the company's
name on Google's screen and no warning.

Today a hosted team cannot do this: every Google page says "Google sign-in comes from the hosting service. Nothing to
set up or upload." (`blocked: 'hosted'`). Self-installed servers can, but card by card.

## Scope

In:
- **Google family only**: Google Drive, Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Docs, Google Sheets. No other connector
  (Notion, Slack, MCP and HTTP connections, …) gets anything new, and the generic connection screens do not change.
- **One company Google app for the whole family**, on a server that signs in through the hosting service (hosted, or
  self-installed and served by it). Set, replaced or removed from any of the five pages; all five follow.
- **Use Trina's Google sign-in again**: removes it; all five go back to the hosting service.
- One guide page in the docs site.

Out:
- Self-installed servers that are not served by the hosting service: their per-card setup stays exactly as it is.
- A different Google app per Google connector on a hosted team (one company app is enough; asked for later, it can be
  added).
- Service accounts, API keys, External apps in Testing mode (one line in the guide only), doing the Google Cloud part
  for the admin, any change to the hosting service or to `shared/src/cloud-protocol/`.

## UI and UX (follows Admin › Team settings)

The five Google pages (`GoogleAdminPage.tsx`) already end with **Team settings**, laid out like Admin › Team settings:
a plain heading, one `SettingRow` per setting (name and one line on the left, its control on the right), hairlines
between, no boxes. The new setting is **one more row there**, first in the list, because it decides how everything
under it signs in. Nothing new above Team settings, no new card, no banner.

**The row, "Google sign-in"** (shared component `GoogleSignInRow`, used by all five pages, built from `SettingRow`
and the `CardMenu` that "Shared Google account" uses):

| State | Line under the name | Control on the right |
|---|---|---|
| Trina's app (default) | "People sign in with Trina's Google app. Your company can use its own Google app instead, for all Google apps here." | Button **Use your company's app** (secondary) |
| Company's app | "People sign in with your company's Google app, for Google Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Docs and Sheets. Set by {name}, {date}." | ⋯ **Replace** · **Use Trina's Google sign-in again** |

- The top note "Google sign-in comes from the hosting service…" goes away on these pages: the row says it, once.
- Team settings shows on a hosted page even before the hosting service answers, so the row is always reachable.
- The row never shows the client ID or secret (as today: set once, never shown again).

**The dialog, "Use your company's Google app"**, in the existing `Modal`, in the step style of "Shared Google
account" (`GoogleAppTeam.tsx`) and Drive's setup (`DriveCloudSteps`). Plain words in our own text; Google's labels
quoted as Google shows them.
1. **Before you start**: who it is for (a company on Google Workspace; with personal Gmail accounts, keep Trina's
   sign-in), about 15 minutes in Google Cloud, a link to the guide.
2. **In Google Cloud**: the numbered steps with a link per Google page, reusing `DriveCloudSteps` with the APIs of all
   five, and the two return addresses to paste into Google, each with **Copy**.
3. **Add it here**: drop or choose the downloaded file; "Enter the ID and secret instead" opens two fields. A file
   that does not list both return addresses is refused with a line saying which one to add.
4. **Before you save**: what changes, with real numbers: "{n} people will connect Google again. Shared Google
   accounts and Drives shared with the team need an admin to reconnect them." Buttons **Save** and **Cancel**.
   Nothing changes before Save.

After Save: a toast, the row shows the company app, the shared rows under it show **Reconnect**. Members' pages show
their usual Reconnect; nothing new for members. **Use Trina's Google sign-in again** asks first with the same numbers.

Checked at 1440 and 390 wide, light and dark, keyboard and screen reader (`ui-polish`, `impeccable`); on a phone the
control goes under the line (`SettingRow` does this) and the dialog fills the screen. Five languages, English first
(`en.ts`); plain-words check (no "OAuth", "client", "redirect URI", "broker" in our own text).

## Steps

1. **Check first (no code).** On a hosted server: `tunnel.host` is the team address, so both return addresses are
   exact (`/api/integrations/google-drive/callback`, `/api/integrations/google-apps/callback`); a Drive `client.json`
   wins over the broker in `googleDriveOf`, and "same as Drive" cards follow it (`setupOf`); the hosted image sets no
   `MYCEL_GOOGLE_*_CLIENT_FILE`; the secrets folder survives updates and moves and is in backups.
2. **Server, one setting for the family** (`server/src/integrations/google-drive/own-app.ts`, new). Stored as today's
   files: the company app is written as Drive's `client.json` (0600, renamed into place, read back) and each of the four
   apps is set to "same as Drive", so the existing sign-in, refresh, revoke and "a new app ends old sign-ins" code runs
   unchanged. Settings row `integrations.googleOwnApp` = `{ key, by, at }`. Allowed only where the hosting service is
   the sign-in (hosted, or served) and no operator client exists; owners and admins only.
3. **API.** `GET /api/admin/google/own-app` → `{ available, active, setBy, setAt, people, shared }` (counts for step 4
   of the dialog); `PUT` with the file or ID and secret (must list both return addresses); `DELETE` (only one this
   setting made). Audits `google.own_app_set` / `google.own_app_removed` without ID or secret. Card-level
   `canSetUp` and `blocked: 'hosted'` stay as they are, so older apps and the header's Set up are unchanged.
   Regenerate `docs/site/reference/http-api.md`.
4. **Web.** `GoogleSignInRow` and its dialog; the row first in Team settings on all five pages; remove the duplicate
   top note where the row shows; reuse `DriveCloudSteps`, `SettingRow`, `CardMenu`, `Modal`, `confirmDialog` (shared
   components, no one-off sizes).
5. **Guide in the Trina AX docs**: `docs/site/use/google-own-app.md` ("Use your company's own Google app"), listed in
   `docs/site/nav.json` under Use Trina AX › Work with the AI, after `use/mcp`. It is part of the docs site
   (`bun run docs:build`) and so of the public copy at `ax.trina.app/docs`, next to the Slack guide, and written like
   it (`docs/site/use/slack.md`): short steps, a screenshot where a step shows a screen. `docs/site/use/admin.md` and
   the Google lines of `use/mcp.md` link to it, and the dialog's "Read the guide" link opens it. For an office IT
   admin:
   1. Who it is for, and what stays the same for members.
   2. Make a project in Google Cloud.
   3. Switch on five APIs: Drive, Gmail, Calendar, Docs, Sheets.
   4. Google Auth Platform › Branding (name, support email) and Audience: **Internal**.
   5. Data access: every scope, as one block to copy.
   6. Clients › Create client › Web application; paste both return addresses copied from Trina; download the file.
   7. In Trina: any Google page › Team settings › Google sign-in › Use your company's app.
   8. Optional: Admin console › Security › API controls, mark the app as trusted.
   9. What Trina keeps and how (a private file on the team server, never shown again; the hosting service runs it);
      going back to Trina's sign-in.
   10. Problems: `redirect_uri_mismatch`, "Some permissions were not ticked", `admin_policy_enforced`, an account from
       outside the company.
   Update the "Hosted" parts of `docs/GOOGLE-APPS.md` and `docs/GOOGLE-DRIVE.md`; a DECISIONS entry.
6. **Screenshots for the guide**, through the docs' own script (`scripts/docs/screenshots.ts`, `bun run
   docs:screenshots`): light theme, 1280×800 at 2×, cropped to what the step talks about, WebP in
   `docs/site/images/google-own-app/`, made-up team "Acme" only (the repository is public). The states come from the

   - `row-trina` — Gmail's page, Team settings, the Google sign-in row with **Use your company's app**
   - `dialog-start`, `dialog-cloud`, `dialog-upload`, `dialog-confirm` — the dialog's four steps
   - `row-company` — the row after Save, with ⋯ open (Replace, Use Trina's Google sign-in again)
   - `member-reconnect` — a member's Gmail page asking to connect again
   - `row-company-phone` — the row at 390 wide
   Every image has alt text that says what is on it, as in the Slack guide. Google Cloud's own screens are not
   captured: they change often and show the signed-in account; the guide quotes Google's labels and links to each page.
7. `changes/google-own-app.md`; a QA sheet row per case below.

## Risks

- **Reverses a decision**: "a hosted server: the hosting service owns its setup" (feat/drive-broker,
  feat/google-one-library). Needs the owner's OK before code.
- **Switching ends sign-ins** for every Google connection here; the dialog shows the numbers before Save, and nothing
  is revoked at Google by the switch (`googleAccountInUse` rule unchanged).
- **Security.** The secret alone opens nothing (each person still consents at Google); kept as today; owners and
  admins only; members get 403. The guide says plainly that the hosting service runs the server.
- **A team address change** breaks the return addresses at Google: the row and the guide say to add the new ones.
- No migration. Other connectors untouched (test below).
