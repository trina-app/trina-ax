# Many pictures in one answer, without "continue in a new message"

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-18 10:44
- Branch: `fix/limits-images`
- Status: done
- Where: trina-ax core, `server/src/proxy/`, `server/src/files/`. Why: the per-answer image cap and the model request live there (see docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md)
- Version impact: patch, no new setting; one answer can look at many more pictures

## Goal

Today one answer may show the AI at most 40 pictures or 10 MB (`files/vision.ts` VISION_TURN_IMAGES/BYTES). Past that
the AI hears "Continue in a new message" and hands the job back to the person (limits audit #4, QA TC-4907). A person
who attaches 60 photos and asks about all of them should get one answer that covers them.

## Scope

- Model proxy (Claude path, every request): fit the pictures that are still in the request. The newest ones go as
  they are; older ones are sent again smaller (768 px, about 150 KB, cached); past what one request may hold (100
  images, about 20 MB of pictures) the oldest give way to a one-line note. The boundary moves in steps of 12 images so
  the cached prompt prefix changes rarely.
- Per-answer caps in the file service: with the proxy fitting the request, only a safety cap (400 pictures / 400 MB a
  turn: conversion time and memory). Without it (Codex, a direct sign-in), the source itself sends pictures smaller
  after the first 16 (or 8 MB), up to 90 a turn / 24 MB.
- The cap note no longer says "continue in a new message": it tells the AI to answer from what it saw and name the
  files it did not look at.
- `read` description: look at many pictures in batches; earlier ones stay at a lower resolution.
- Attachments per message 20 -> 100 (60 photos must fit in one message).

Not in scope: Codex request fitting (Responses format), video frame sizes.

## Tests

- Unit: fitting a request (stable boundary, shrink, drop with note, nested tool_result images, failures forward the
  request unchanged); per-turn caps by mode; the note's wording; attachments max.
- Real AI: 60 photos attached, one question about all of them, one answer.

## Result

Built as planned; decision in docs/decisions/20261118-limits-images.md. Real AI (Claude Code on a copy of the QA team, 60 attached photos, one question): 60 of 60 named in one answer, read in batches of 10, both without the proxy (dev sign-in: pictures after the first 16 sent smaller) and through it (office mode: the proxy sent 48 of 60 older pictures smaller in the last request).
