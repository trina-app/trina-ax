# A seal on a PDF lands on the printed mark, under the same rules as Word and Hangul

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-23 20:36

- Status: in progress (plan reviewed and revised; ownership part waits on a decision)
- Where: Team AX core (`server/src/files`, `server/src/tools`, `shared/src/tools.ts`). Why: the PDF tools and the signing path are core (docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md)
- Version impact: minor, `stamp_pdf` gains an `at` input

## Goal

PR 2 of docs/decisions/20261123-signing-slots.md. Today the AI must work out x/y for a seal on a PDF from a page image, and
any picture goes on with no ownership check. Word and Hangul have a shared target resolver, a card for a picture that is not
the person's own, and a result that says what was checked. PDF gets the same.

## What was measured before planning

- `pdftotext -bbox -cropbox` (Poppler is on every install, `packages.json`) gives word boxes **in the page as shown**, from its
  top-left, for /Rotate 0, 90, 180, 270 and with a CropBox (8 made-up PDFs). That is the coordinate system `stamp_pdf` already
  takes as `x_mm`/`y_mm`. Its page width and height are **not** swapped for 90 and 270, so they are not used.
- It reads text in Form XObjects and real font widths, which `pdf-text.ts` does not (that reader estimates `Uni*-UCS2-H`).
- Drawing order is not reading order, so `occurrence` cannot count in drawing order.

## Independent plan review (verdict: rework), and what changed

Reproduced on made-up PDFs: a hidden name can bind a signer to another person's visible mark; a label across lines can pair a
name with the neighbour's mark; pdf-lib and Poppler can read different objects, so the saved file can say something else; a
check using the stamper's own box passes when the seal is 35 mm off or not visible at all; and x/y lets the AI step around any
refusal. The design below replaces the first draft.

## Design (PR 2a: \`stamp_pdf\` only)

1. **Input.** Optional \`at\` and \`occurrence\` on \`stamp_pdf\`; \`at\` excludes \`position\`. \`page\` limits the search. Default width 15 mm.
   The PDF must have been read in this chat, at these exact bytes.
2. **Model, \`server/src/files/pdf-marks.ts\`.** Words and boxes from \`pdftotext -bbox-layout -cropbox\`, with its own time, memory and
   output limits. Explicit line bands, then left to right. Rotated or upside-down candidates are refused.
3. **Target.** One line only: the name and the mark adjacent on it. No cross-line labels. A label with no recognised mark is refused
   (PDF has no older behaviour to keep). Marks are normalised per word with an offset map. A mark glued to other letters is refused.
4. **Visible ink.** Every word box of the label and the mark must show rendered ink, and no other word may overlap them. Hidden,
   white, covered or clipped text is refused, including on OCR layers.
5. **Same document.** Words and boxes of all pages are compared before and after. Any difference saves nothing.
6. **Landing, by rendering.** The page is rendered before and after with \`pdftoppm\`, isolated. The changed pixels must be centred within
   2 mm of the mark, with the expected visible area. Otherwise nothing is saved. No "not checked" save for \`at\`.
7. **Boxes.** CropBox is intersected with MediaBox in both stamp paths.
8. **No way around.** After an \`at\` refusal on these bytes, x/y and corner stamps of that PDF are refused for the rest of the turn; the AI
   asks the person which place.
9. **Refusals.** At most 5 places, as capped, quoted document text.

## Decision needed

Giving PDF the ownership card means a scheduled run that stamps a shared company seal is refused unless that person chose Always, and
a scheduled run cannot offer Always. The quote flow FL-08 gains a card.

## Scope

Out: scanned PDFs with no text layer, signature boxes without printed text, marks in annotations or form fields.

## Risks

Tool schema changes: exact tool lists and engine prompt pins re-recorded on purpose. Rendering adds time per call. Poppler's minimum
version (22.12) must be run on the corpus.

## Tests

Numeric corpus: whole line, two signers on a line, skewed lines, duplicated marks, multi-page, four rotations, CropBox larger than
MediaBox, Form XObject, hidden and white text, covered marks, fake bold, glued marks, Korean CID fonts, duplicate object numbers. A
generated test: the true target or a refusal. \`typecheck\`, PDF suites, \`check:prepush\` on Bun 1.3.12.
