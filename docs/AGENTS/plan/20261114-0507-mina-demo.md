# Hands-on demo: the real web app in the browser with a scripted backend ("미나의 첫날")

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-14 05:07
- Branch: `feat/mina-demo`
- Status: done (PR #807, not merged)
- Where: web/ (`web/demo/`, a separate Vite mode). Why: it is the web client running against a fake backend in the
  page; nothing on the server changes and nothing of it ships in the normal bundle.
- Version impact: none. The production build is unchanged; the demo is a separate static build.

## Goal

A visitor of the website tries Trina AX hands-on without an account: "미나", a new marketing hire at a fictional
company, goes through her first day in about 10 clicks (3 chapters, English first). Every visitor gets a fresh copy in
their own tab; it can be hosted as static files (no server, no AI calls, no database).

## Approach: run the e2e mock server in the page

`web/test/mock/server.ts` (the e2e mock) already answers the whole REST API and the WebSocket protocol, checks every
answer against the shared contract schemas and is kept in step with the contract by the e2e suite. The demo runs that
same class in the browser instead of porting its handlers:

- `bun run build:demo` = `vite build --mode demo` to `web/dist-demo`. In that mode only, index.html loads
  `web/demo/main.tsx` instead of `src/main.tsx`, and the mock's few Bun/Node needs are aliased to small browser shims
  (`web/demo/shims/`: sha256, `Bun.hash`, an fs/os/path/zlib stand-in; the sealer and serveWeb are not used).
- `web/demo/main.tsx` builds a `DemoServer` (a subclass of `MockServer`), seeds it from the content, then answers
  `fetch('/api/…')` in the page and a fake `WebSocket` at `/api/ws`, then loads the normal `src/main.tsx`.
  Pictures and downloads (`<img src="/api/…">`) go through a small service worker that asks the page for the answer.
- The subclass replaces only: the seed (people, rooms, DMs, projects, files from the content), the AI turn (the
  script engine instead of the mock's keywords), and the room messages the mock does not keep (send, list, events).
- Rejected: porting the handlers to a new browser module (thousands of lines that would drift from the contract), and
  MSW (a dependency, and it does not cover the WebSocket protocol or the contract checks).

Hosting: static files at the root of an origin (the app's routes are absolute), over https (the worker needs a secure
context), with unknown paths answered by index.html (a reload of `/rooms/…` lands on the app, which starts over at
Home). Nothing is cached between visits; every tab is a fresh copy.

## Content format

`web/demo/script.ts`: TypeScript types for one story (people, groups, rooms with earlier messages, projects with past
chats, files, the canned answers keyed by their prompts, persona messages with triggers, the guide line, the end
card), with an example in `web/demo/example/`. The real story lives in `scripts/demo/mina/` (written on
`feat/mina-demo-content`), English first (owner, 2026-11-14: Trina is a global service). The build picks the language
with `DEMO_LOCALE` (default en): `scripts/demo/mina/index.<locale>.ts` when there, else `index.ts`; the app's own
words follow the story's language. ko, id, ja, es come later as content only.

## Scope

In: the demo build, the script engine, the free-typing reply (offers the suggested questions as buttons), the guide
line in a corner ("다음: … →" that highlights the next control and brings a visitor back), reset ("처음부터" and a
reload), the end card (sign-up link configurable with `VITE_DEMO_SIGNUP_URL`), a Playwright walk of the story at 1440
and 390.
Out: hosting/deploy, permissions UI and admin pages (the visitor is a member, so they are not offered), any real AI.

## Steps

1. Plan and content types first (pushed early so the content agent can follow them).
2. Vite demo mode, shims, fetch/WebSocket/service-worker bridge to a `DemoServer`.
3. Seed from content, script engine (answers, ask cards, persona events), rooms in the subclass.
4. Guide line, suggestions, end card, reset. The demo's two own words are in `web/demo/words.ts`, not `src/i18n`, so the product's locale files (and bundle) stay the same.
5. Playwright walk at 1440 and 390, screenshots light and dark.

## Risks

- The production bundle must not change: the demo is reached only through `--mode demo` (checked by building both).
- The mock's code is test code; running it in a browser must not need changes that make the e2e weaker.
- Phone width: the guide line must not cover the composer.
- No real people or companies in the content; every person is a member (DECISIONS "Demo company").

## v2 (owner rejected v1 on 2026-11-14: "send it to #Marketing" was unclear, no impact, no photos)

Nine clicks, four wow beats: the structured company answer (sources, people with photos) and Sara's toast; the
project's banner with two big prompts and an answer with five deliverables (before/after slider, square, story,
captions, a fifth file) and ONE "Send to #Marketing" button that posts in one click and opens the room; the room comes
alive (typing, the CEO and Leo, reactions piling up, presence dots, ✦ glowing); the side chat's one prompt, a draft with
its attachments and a two-button confirm card, then the agency's toast and the end card with the day's numbers.
Format additions are all optional (`web/demo/script.ts`): outside people, inviter, sources, people, compare,
deliverables, sendTo, attachments, toasts, typingMs, reactions, `away` lines, end stats. Clarity: exactly one control
glows and the guide line points at it; the guide line does its step (presses the control, sends a single prompt, or
takes the visitor there); a "…" pill between steps; prompts go once used; on phones the guide is a bottom bar of its
own and the app gets the rest of the height. Avatars are image files in the story's assets (initials until replaced).

## Result

v2 built and walked: the Playwright walk passes 24/24 (both branches, 1440 and 390, light and dark); a newcomer walker reached the end card on every round and reported no new problems in its last round; the production build is byte-identical to dev.

## v3: the app's own UI only (owner, 2026-11-14: "우리 실제 UI는 다 버리고 저따구로 하냐")

Every demo-made card, toast, button, tile, slider and end modal is gone. Answers show their pictures inline as a real
answer shows team-file images, with the app's own sources and saved-files rows; sending is the app's own Send to room
and its dialog; Sara's DM arrives as a room message (the app's unread badge); Chris's reply is passed on in #Marketing;
the end is the guide line's last state. The demo layer is the guide line (with Start over and the step's prompts) and
a ring over the next real control, nothing else. Links to chats, rooms and projects stay inside the app, since a
reload starts the story over.

## v3.1: one prompt, a base path, the CSP

- One prompt: when a step offers a single prompt, the guide line sends it (no choice buttons). The story has one
  Higgsfield path now; the photo campaign is JPEG at quality 85 (4:4:4) to keep the public demo light.
- Hosting at https://ax.trina.app/demo/: `DEMO_BASE=/demo/ bun run build:demo`. The app keeps its own addresses;
  web/demo/base.ts adds the base when the app writes an address and takes it off where the app reads one (its history
  gets a window whose location shows the app's address, through a demo-only source transform in web/demo/vite.ts).
  /api stays at the root (the page answers it), the worker lives at `<base>sw.js` with the base as its scope and
  fetches the app's root public files (/favicon.svg, /icons/…) from under the base. The site serves index.html for
  every address under the base that is not a file. Without DEMO_BASE it builds for the root as before.
- The CSP the demo needs (web/test/demo/serve.ts DEMO_CSP; the walk runs under it and fails on a violation):
  `default-src 'self'; script-src 'self'; style-src 'self' 'unsafe-inline'; img-src 'self' blob: data:;
  font-src 'self' data:; media-src 'self' blob: data:; connect-src 'self' blob: data:; worker-src 'self' blob:;
  frame-src 'self' blob:; manifest-src 'self'; object-src 'none'; base-uri 'self'; form-action 'self';
  frame-ancestors 'self'`.
