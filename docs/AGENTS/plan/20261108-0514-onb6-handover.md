# Onboarding v6: easy owner handover (팀장 넘기기)

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-08 05:14
- Branch: `feat/onb6-handover`
- Status: done
- Where: Team AX core (`server/src/admin`, `server/src/auth`, `shared/src/api`, `web/src`). Why: owner permissions,
  invites and notices are core (docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md).
- Version impact: minor. A new invite flag, a handover card and notices, new endpoints and a migration; the transfer
  endpoint keeps its shape and adds optional fields.

## Goal

The first account stays the owner and nobody is asked "팀장이세요?". When the person who set the team up is not its lead
(often our own staff setting up a customer's team), handing the team over is easy and leaves them no hidden access:

1. the owner ticks "이 사람이 팀장이에요" on the invite and says who it is for ("누구에게 보내나요?");
2. when that person joins, the owner gets a card on Home, an Inbox notice and a push, with facts the joiner cannot set;
3. one confirm dialog (card, Inbox, Admin › 멤버 › ··· › 팀장 넘기기) moves the role with the password, and ends the
   connections made with the leaver's own accounts that they leave ticked;
4. both people, the other admins and every open tab hear about it at once.

## Scope

In:
- The transfer race (two owners): one `.immediate()` transaction, caller and target read again inside it, exactly one
  row changed per role swap, 409 "이미 팀장이 바뀌었어요" otherwise. Target rules: active, not me, past the temporary
  password, signed in at least once.
- The lead flag on per-person invites: owner only (server), member in the team group, 3 days, one open (a new one
  revokes the old in one write, partial unique index), cleared by a transfer; `leadFor` ≤ 60 characters, plain text.
- The card (Home top slot) and the Inbox notice with a desktop notification and a phone / browser push; ✕ hides the
  card only; the card holds only while the flagging owner is still the owner and the joiner is active.
- The confirm dialog: 받는 사람, what moves, the leaver's own connections (team AI sign-ins, Drive team shares; API keys
  not listed), each ticked; ended after the commit (Google revocation after the commit, never rolling back the
  transfer); the password (5 per 15 min, an Inbox note to the owner on lockout); 409 text.
- After: realtime role refresh for both people, notices to the new owner ([내보내기] = the existing disable), the old
  owner and the other admins, audit rows for each step, the old owner's admin invites revoked (as today).
- Admin › 멤버: "팀장 넘기기" moves from Admin › 팀 설정 into each person's ··· menu (owner only); Account deletion's
  link goes to Admin › 멤버.
- Hosted teams: no flag and no card until the control plane refuses owner/admin reset links (`handoverSafe`, a
  server.json key the operator sets); only ··· › 팀장 넘기기, and both people are told who can still reset passwords.
  The tenant side of the refusal: with `handoverSafe` on, a transfer sets a recovery lock in its transaction, and
  `mycel-ctl reset-link` refuses every owner or admin target in both forms while it is set.

Out:
- "넘긴 뒤 나는 이 팀에서 나가요" (owner: dropped). The helper's way out is the new owner's [내보내기] and Admin › 멤버.
- Higgsfield and team MCP connections in the dialog (owner: no Higgsfield now).
- The control plane's report intake, claim link and recovery contact (hosted side; listed in the PR).

## Result

Built as planned; DECISIONS "W6" has the details. What differs:
- The migration is `270_b_owner_handover.sql` (number 270, in the 2xx range the migration test requires).
- From the independent security review: a temporary-password reset racing the transfer could land on the new owner
  (reset and member changes now re-read caller and target inside an immediate write); CLI-made reset links for either
  person stop at the transfer and none works for an owner or admin under the hosted lock; the lockout note covers every
  password re-check of the owner and is pushed (a second push event, `owner.reauth_locked`); a transfer stops the open
  lead link; only the owner stops a lead link; the joiner's name is shown as one plain line (and isolated in the web).
- `handoverSafe` must stay off until the control plane can run `owner-recovery-claimed` (not yet a tenant command).
- The route-level reauth hook became `AuthService.onReauthLocked`; `http/route.ts` is unchanged.
