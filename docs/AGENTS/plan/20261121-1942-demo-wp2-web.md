## Goal

A visitor of the free-roam demo (demo.trina.app) can read what is practice on a phone, finds a quiet way to sign up,
gets the first action under Home's message box, keeps most of a 390 px screen, reads one honest question counter to the
end, and never sees the previous visitor's unsent draft.

## Result

Built as planned, with one change to the bar on phones: measured at 390 px in all 6 languages, two rows cannot hold
who you are, the count, the practice label and Start free (es and id: who you are alone is 316-323 px of the 366 px,
and the count plus the practice label is 390-407 px). So, since ", a made-up company" is never cut, a phone shows
three short rows inside the same 56 px: who you are; the count with Start free at its end; the practice label. One
line on a computer. The e2e checks the 56 px and the rows in every language.

Home's chips on a phone sit above the box, which is docked at the bottom there, like Home's other suggestions. On a
computer they sit under the box.

The `?fresh=1` cleanup runs when GET /api/demo says free roam. App.tsx shows its splash until then, so no composer has
read a draft or the send queue yet. The gate's redirect is WP1 (#287).
