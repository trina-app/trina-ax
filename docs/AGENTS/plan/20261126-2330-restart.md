# Restart Trina from Admin, and a watchdog for a server that stops answering

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-26 23:30
- Branch: `feat/restart-app` (server, web, office supervisor) and `feat/restart-root` (launcher op, bootstrap watchdog)
- Status: done
- Where: server/src/system, server/src/routes, web/src/pages/admin, server/src/office, native/cmd/mycel-launcher,
  native/cmd/mycel-bootstrap. Why: a hung answer or a hung server needed SSH (`systemctl restart`) or a CP reboot.
- Version impact: minor (0.21.0), a new feature; a root release (the bootstrap and the launcher in `root/` change).
  The app part works without the root part: "AI만" stays hidden until the launcher advertises the op.

## Goal

An owner or admin whose AI answers hang, or whose app is slow or stuck, can restart the AI or the whole app from
Admin › System › 상태 without SSH. A server that stops answering at all is restarted by its supervisor.

## Scope

In:
- One "다시 시작…" row in the Health card (owner) and the same row alone for admins. The dialog: what (AI만 /
  {PRODUCT} 전체), when ("답변이 끝나면", at most 10 minutes; owners also "지금 바로"), then the password or Google check.
- `server/src/system/restart.ts`: refusals (ops lock, update waiting or under way, restore prepared, state.json pending
  or forward equals the running version, app/current is not the running version), a rate limit on accepted requests
  (audit rows, every kind together, 5 per hour and 2 minutes apart), the wait with its own `kind: 'restart'` on the
  update-wait marker and its own wording, "지금 바로" and "그만두기" while it waits, audit `system.restart_requested`
  and `system.restarted`.
- The server option only with `MYCEL_SUPERVISOR_PID` set (the bootstrap) or in office mode. "AI만" only with an installed
  launcher whose status lists `restart` in `ops`.
- Launcher op `{"op":"restart"}`: refuse new turns, end live turns with reason `restart`, exit (systemd
  `Restart=always`, launchd KeepAlive, `mycel-ctl supervise` start it again). Advertised in status `ops`.
- Bootstrap watchdog: after this child answered health ok once, every 30 s the same `checkHealth` (3 s timeout); 10
  failures in a row (about 5 minutes) is a hang. Kill switch `watchdog: {kill: true}` in server.json, off by default
  (log only). Migrating is not counted; pending and forwarded versions keep their own watch; exit 75 and `block()`
  unchanged. At most 3 kills per hour, then `state.json liveness.gaveUp` until the next healthy answer. Shown in
  `mycel-ctl status --detail` and Admin Health.
- Office supervisor: the same rule in TypeScript (`server/src/office/watchdog.ts`), the same kill switch in
  office's server.json.

Out (cut from v1 by the review): the team "컴퓨터까지" CP intake and its contract, launcher WatchdogSec, the
`restart_trina` AI tool, gaveUp in the CP status report (TenantStatus stays as it is). The CP ops reboot button is
trina-cloud work, not in these PRs.

## Risks

- A restart during an update's health check would count toward a roll-back: refused while state.json pending or
  forward equals the running version.
- The watchdog could kill a busy but healthy server: log only by default, 5 minutes, first-ok guard, migrating not
  counted, 3 kills per hour then gaveUp.
- No migration. No change to the CP contract.

## Result

Built as planned, in two PRs into dev (app: server, web, office supervisor; root: launcher op, bootstrap watchdog,
`mycel-ctl status`). Differences:
- No job for the restart (the jobs table's kind CHECK would need a migration): an in-memory waiting restart, read by
  `GET /api/admin/restart` and polled by the row every 5 s while it waits. `POST /api/admin/restart/now` (owner) and
  `DELETE /api/admin/restart` (owner or admin) end or call off the wait.
- The office kill switch is `TEAMAX_WATCHDOG_KILL=1` in `config/office.env`, not server.json: office rewrites
  server.json at every start. Its give-up goes to `data/liveness.json`, which Health reads in office mode.
- The CP ops reboot button (trina-cloud) is not in these PRs.
