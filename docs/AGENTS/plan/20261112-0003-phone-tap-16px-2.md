## Goal

A check on a real phone (Galaxy, Chrome, 411 px) found controls still under 44 px after #487, and a PDF zoomed with a
pinch or a double tap moves sideways by the pages' 16 px inset × (scale − 1).

## Scope

In: the side chat's starter chips; a Files row's ⋯ menu items and checkbox; the Files search results and the search
box's ✕ (its tap box reached over New folder); Settings' theme buttons and text-size slider; My usage's period and
"Everyone's usage"; the invite page's "Copy invite & get the app" and "Continue in the browser"; Admin's "Learn more"
(System and the shared Setting row); Members' ⌄ beside Invite; the file picker's ✕ (placed with absolute: a new
`touch-hit-placed`, its place kept). The PDF's scroll left after a zoom scales only the page, not the inset beside it.
Out: Scheduled's header search ✕: a phone never shows it (a field of its own in the page).

## Steps

1. Phone-only classes as #487 did (`max-md:min-h-11`, `touch-hit`; the 1440 layout unchanged).
2. `lib/pdfPages.ts` `scrollLeftAfterZoom`, used by PdfView; unit tests.
3. `phone-targets.spec.ts` measures each item (scrolled into view, in its menu); `phone-previews-fit.spec.ts`
   asserts the horizontal anchor too (within 5 px).

## Risks

A `touch-hit` box over a neighbour (checked by the spec's hit test); computers must not change (all classes are
below 768 px).

## Tests
