## Goal

Onboarding v6.2 (owner decisions of 2026-11-08): nothing goes into a team without a press that names it, and setup ends
with one press to make a first result instead of a deck built in the background.

- 회사 소개 saves the profile only. Its 저장하고 계속 puts the website's pictures and 회사 소개.md into the team's brand
  folder (no AI, no chat, no skill). It counts as answered once saved or put off.
- A new step 도구 연결 between 팀 파일 and 팀원 초대: the Google Drive row where this server has Drive, and one quiet
  "다른 도구 연결하기 (MCP)" line that opens today's add-a-connection dialog. Every team gets it (n/5, n/6 with AI 연결);
  a phone skips it and records it as put off.

  글); it opens that skill's form only.
