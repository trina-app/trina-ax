## Goal

QA TC-258 (P2) and the Files row ⋯ part of TC-249. The owner, on Files › Team folder › Groups › leads: "여기 UI가 모바일
친화적임?". On a phone it is the desktop explorer squeezed: three or four header rows before the first file (on a long
path the first row starts at y≈318), no ‹ or folder title (FILES-02/03), rows with the name only (FILES-17), "팀 폴더"
twice (FILES-18), the trash with raw storage paths and a four-line paragraph (FILES-19), a long press to select that
nobody finds and its actions at the top (FILES-27). The row ⋯ opens a small desktop menu that runs under the tab bar
near the end of a list (FILES-04), and from 640 px it shows only on hover, so a tablet never sees it (FILES-21).

## Scope

In (below 768 px only; computers and tablets look the same as before):
- One bar (`PhonePageBar`): ‹ to the folder above with its name ("‹ 파일" at a top folder), the folder's name as the
  title, ⊕ with New folder / Upload as a sheet (only where the folder takes new files). At the team folder: no ‹, the
  title "파일". The ‹ calls the screen's own `go()` (lib/screenHistory.ts, #457, makes it a Back).
- The search box is the first thing in the list, so it scrolls away; the folder's "who sees it" chip and read-only
  badge go under it. No breadcrumbs and no Team folder / Trash pills on phones.
- Rows of two lines: the name, then "date · size" (a folder: the date), 44 px or more.
- The trash is a place: a "휴지통" row at the end of the team folder's list opens it (the same `setView` call); inside,
  ‹ goes back to the team folder. Its rows say the name and the folder in words (never `groups/leads/…`); a one-line
  note in place of the paragraph; the restore toast names the folder in words.
- Selecting: "선택" in each row's ⋯ starts it (the long press stays); the selection's actions sit at the bottom, above
  the tab bar.
- The row ⋯ is `rooms/MenuButton` at every width (a sheet on phones, the app's menu on computers), with #417's
  rename / move / trash turned off with the reason, and the download-only reason. MenuButton items gain `disabled` and
  `describedBy`, and the menu a `footer` for the reason. The ⋯ hides until hover only where the pointer can hover.

- Phones: two or more finished uploads fold into one line (FILES-25), in `FileUploads.tsx` (render only; once #433, the
  uploads session's PR, had landed and no open PR touched it).

Out:
- Previews and search by name (TC-259, TC-260): another session.
- How Back and ‹ navigate (TC-244, #457) and the empty-folder wording (TC-207).
