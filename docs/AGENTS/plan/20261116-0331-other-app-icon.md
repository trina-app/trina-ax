# Other apps in conversations: a letter by default, and an icon people can change

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-16 03:31
- Branch: `feat/other-app-icon`
- Status: done
- Where: trina-ax (Team AX core: server/src/rooms/apps, web/src/components/rooms/apps). Why: apps posting in rooms are
  a core chat feature (see docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md)
- Version impact: minor, a new setting on an existing feature (three endpoints, one additive migration)
- QA: TC-3544 (the letter by default), TC-3545 (Change icon and who may change it)

## Goal

An "Other app" (a Webhook URL with a name the person gives, such as "Dr Alaseo Alert") shows one grey blocks icon, so
two or three Other apps in one conversation look the same. Owner decision 2026-11-16: an Other app without an icon shows
its name's first letter on a tile with a stable colour from the app's id; its settings get one "Change icon" action
(upload an image or pick an emoji; "Remove icon" goes back to the letter). Preset apps keep their logos.

## Scope

In:
- The letter tile: `AppTile` draws an Other app's first letter (as `Avatar` does for a person without a photo) on a tint
  picked from the app id by one small shared palette (light and dark). A removed app keeps its letter (from its name).
- Storage: a new table `room_app_icons` (migration 356, component C range as asked): one row per app with the image
  bytes and type, or one emoji, and a version (the avatar's ever-growing millisecond clock). `ON DELETE CASCADE` from
  `room_apps`: removing the app (or its room) removes its icon. No user column (account removal is unaffected).
- Upload rules: the profile photo's (`prepareAvatar`): PNG, JPEG or WebP only (never SVG), header must match the type,
  a square of 64–512 px, at most 256 KiB, no animation, every metadata block cut out. The browser crops the centre
  square and scales it to 256 px first (`squarePhoto`, as Settings › Profile does).
- Endpoints: `PUT /api/rooms/:id/apps/:appId/icon?type=` (image), `PUT /api/rooms/:id/apps/:appId/icon/emoji`
  (`{ emoji }`, one emoji by the status/reaction rule), `DELETE /api/rooms/:id/apps/:appId/icon`, and
  `GET /api/rooms/:id/apps/:appId/icon?v=` (the image). Writes follow the existing settings permission exactly
  (`RoomApps.mine`: a team room's owners and admins in it, a 1:1 or group chat's members, never a guest; 403 / 404 as
  the other settings). Only plain Other apps (a preset, Sentry, GitHub, Google Forms: 400). Audited
  (`room_app.icon`). The image is served to anyone in the room (`Rooms.mine`, 404 otherwise), with the avatar's
  image-only headers, cached for a year at the current version.
- The messages' sender (`RoomAppRef`) and the app (`RoomApp`) carry `icon` (`{ image }` with the versioned path, or
  `{ emoji }`) from the live app, so past messages show the new icon too; the changer's open timeline updates at once.
- Webhook payloads: Slack's `username`, `icon_url`, `icon_emoji` and Discord's `avatar_url` stay ignored (a test).
- Settings: one "Change icon" button by the name (Other only): Upload an image, Pick an emoji, Remove icon.
- Copy in en.ts first, then ko and the other tables; features.ts, docs:api, the user guide page, a decision file, a
  changes fragment.

Out:
- A per-message icon or name from the payload (impersonation risk, owner decision 3).
- Icons for preset apps, Sentry, GitHub, Google Forms.
- A realtime event to other people's open windows: they see the new icon when the conversation loads again.

## Risks

- Image upload: the same checks as profile photos (server-side, no image library). Served with `nosniff` and a
  sandbox CSP, members of the room only.
- Permission: exactly the settings' rule (no new rule).
- Migration number: 355 is the highest of C on dev; 356 is free on dev and in open PRs.

## Tests

under Bun 1.3.12, web unit tests for the tile, targeted e2e `room-apps.spec.ts` and a new `room-apps-icon.spec.ts`.

## Result

Built as planned. Differences:
- The emoji picker is placed under the whole icon row (by the narrow button it ran out of the dialog on a computer).
- The image is readable by guests in the room too (guest-endpoints.ts: they see the messages); managing stays 403.
- People have no colour palette (their letter tile is neutral since 2026-11-05), so the tints are new and for apps only
  (`web/src/lib/appTone.ts`); the letter itself is the person's (`firstLetter`, shared with Avatar.tsx).
