## Goal

Five rough edges a non-developer met in QA:

1. TC-231: after a dropped connection, a page that could not load stays on "Reload to get the current version" until
   clicked, and the owner's goal card on Home stays gone until a reload.
2. TC-232: the folder access confirm says people who join later "can open or edit" it when the change only lets them
   view.
3. TC-233: the "Please check" card for a connected service's read after a room read shows `Action: Api request` and
   the raw `connection: team_x / method: GET / path: /tasks`.
4. TC-234: an admin who picks a switched-off AI is told "Your admin turned this AI off".
5. TC-235: a member who sees a broken team connector is told to check its address, which only an admin can do.

## Scope

In:

1. `ChunkErrorBoundary`: when the page part failed while the server was out of reach, it says it loads by itself once
   the connection is back, and when the live connection is back it reloads by itself if nothing would be lost (the same
   check and the same once-per-5-minutes guard as now); otherwise the Reload button stays. Home's goal card
   (`HomeGoal`) reads the goal again by itself after a connection failure (the `useAutoRetry` of `LoadError`: backoff,
   and at once when the live connection is back), and a failed read keeps the card it had.
2. The edit preview's `latent` says per kind (whoever joins the team, people switched off, whoever joins the groups)
   whether they would get edit somewhere or only view (`latent.edit`, optional). The confirm says "open" for view and
   keeps "open or edit" for edit (and for an answer without the field). Korean lines of the panel with a name before
   "이(가)" or "은(는)" are reworded so no particle depends on the name.
3. A connection tool may give the room card its own words (`TurnTool.card`). `api_request`: "Read from {service}" for
   GET and HEAD, "Change something in {service}" for other methods, the address it calls, the body's values as lines,
   and the exact request one click away ("Show exactly what is sent", `ConfirmDetail.exact`).
4. `engineOff` for the owner and admins: "This AI is turned off for the team. Turn it on in Admin › AI accounts, or
   pick another one." Members keep their line.
5. A connection's last error for someone who cannot fix it: the address problems (unreachable, a private address, a
   port, a redirect, not an MCP server) for whoever does not manage the address (a member on a team connection, a
   member's connection made from a team template), and the key or sign-in problems for a member on a team connection,
   say what happened and "Tell your admin." No names.
6. The address card's "all websites" line (#351) and the two Settings hints next to it use the words of #348's lines
   ("긴 글자" in Korean) and no "token", in all five languages, with the same meaning.

Out: other screens' wording; a Korean particle helper for arbitrary names.

## Risks

- No migration. The API change is one optional field; an older server's answer reads as before.
- The automatic reload after a reconnect uses the same guard as the chunk reload: never while something typed, an
  upload, a recording or an unsent message would be lost, at most once per 5 minutes in a tab.
- The room card for api_request shows the full address it calls (as the connection's own card already does), with
  stored secrets hidden.
- Five languages for every new line; Korean without name-dependent particles.

## Result

PR #397 (https://github.com/trina-app/trina-ax/pull/397). Built as planned, with these changes after the independent
review:

- Item 1: after the connection is back, a reload held back by something that ends by itself (an answer that was
  streaming, a draft) is asked again every 3 seconds; the "loads by itself" line shows only when the 5-minute guard
  allows a reload. A reply typed in a room's box counts as a draft (also when its box is off screen), for this and the
  update reload.
- Item 3: the address on the card is the one the person may see (a team admin's query left out), and the connection's
  own write card now does the same. A read says "Address", a change "Sends to".
- Item 4: the admin wording only when it is Admin's switch that is off.
- Korean: one pattern for every "who" line ("열 수 있게 되는 사람: Dan").
- Left as is: one latent line per kind ("open or edit" for all of a kind when one of them gets edit); the room card's
  lead line ("changes are asked first") on a read.
