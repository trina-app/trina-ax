# A leaner 활동 panel: one filter line, an Unreads switch, two-line rows

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-27 13:38
- Branch: `plan/activity-lean` (plan only; the build branch would be `ui/activity-lean`)
- Status: draft
- Where: trina-ax `web/` (the computer's panel `components/panels/ActivityPanel.tsx`, `lib/activityKinds.ts`, the
  phone page `pages/Activity.tsx`, i18n). Why: the panel and its chips are the web app's (see
  docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md). No server, API or migration change.
- Version impact: minor. A visible change to a main navigation list (chips, sections and rows), no API change.

## Goal

The owner, looking at the 활동 panel: "야 이거 너무 번잡하긴 하네. 이거 슬랙이랑 똑같이 갈려고 했는데.. 어카지?"

The panel next to the rail is 240 px wide on a 1052–1200 px window and 288 px wide at 1440
(`PanelFrame`: `clamp(240px, min(18rem, 20vw), 300px)`). Today it shows:

- a header with the title, "Mark all as read" (only while unread) and a gear that links to Settings › Notifications;
- six chips, 전체 · DM · 멘션 · 스레드 · 트리나 · 요청, that wrap onto two lines at both widths (`flex-wrap`);
- the unread rows, then a folding "읽음" section header with the read rows under it;
- conversation rows of up to three lines: a 28 px photo, "name: message" clamped to two lines, then
  "#place › 스레드 · time".

The goal is a panel that a person who knows Slack reads at a glance: one filter line, one way to hide read items,
and rows of two lines at most. It keeps the "One Activity" decision
([20261126-one-activity.md](../../decisions/20261126-one-activity.md)): every notice stays in 활동, and only the
controls get lighter.

What this means for us. Slack itself now shows only two filters by default and keeps the rest behind a menu. It has
no gear in Activity, and it offers a "Dense" layout for people who want short rows. Our copy stays our own (the owner's rule, 2026-11-10: English first, not a Slack copy):
we keep Slack's ideas, not its words or its saved-views system.

## What the chips hold today (origin/dev)

- **DM** (`activityTabHas` 'dms'): a mention, or a reply in a thread I follow, inside a DM or group chat. The server
  lists only mentions and followed-thread replies (`rooms/inbox.ts` `LISTED_SQL`). So **every row under DM is also
  under 멘션 or 스레드**. A plain DM message is never a notice; it is in 대화. The chip adds nothing, and its empty state
  has to explain why DMs are missing (`feed.dmsEmpty`).
- **트리나**: what the AI did by itself, such as a scheduled task's run (`schedule.success`, `schedule.failure`) or a
  project's job (`dev_env.job`, `dev_env.box`).
- **요청**: something waits for my answer: a suggested skill to review, a request to add an app or connect the AI, an
  account deletion request, a reconnect, skills to merge, or a request to open my file, chat or project. Owners and
  admins see it; a member sees it only while one of their requests is listed. These are rare and urgent, and they are
  also under 전체.

## Decisions in this plan

### 1. One filter line that never wraps

The visible chips are **전체 · 멘션 · 스레드**, then **"더 보기"**, a small chip with a "⋯" icon and the tooltip and
label "More filters". Its menu lists 트리나, and 요청 when that person has it.

- **DM is removed.** Every DM row is already under 멘션 or 스레드, and DM messages live in 대화. Slack has a DM filter
  because Slack lists every DM message in Activity. We decided not to do that (one-activity decision, "Left out").
- **트리나 and 요청 move into the "⋯" menu.** When one of them is picked, the "⋯" chip turns into a pressed chip with
  its name and a caret, such as "요청 ▾". The person can see which filter is on, and opening the menu again lets them
  go back. The menu marks the chosen item with a check. 전체 is not repeated in the menu: it is always a visible chip.
- **A dot on a chip** (as the phone's tabs have) marks an unread item under 멘션, 스레드 or "⋯" (that is, under 트리나
  or 요청). A pending request is not hidden by the menu: the "⋯" chip shows the dot.
- **It never wraps.** Use `flex-nowrap` with `shrink-0` chips. On a 240 px panel in a long language (es, id), the row
  can scroll sideways by a few pixels as a last resort (`overflow-x-auto`, thin scroll bar), and the selected chip is
  scrolled into view. Widths, measured at the panel's 232 px inside width: ko 전체 44 + 멘션 48 + 스레드 52 + ⋯ 28 +
  gaps 12 = 184 px. en All 44 + Mentions 72 + Threads 66 + ⋯ 28 + 12 = 222 px. Both fit at 240 px. With "Requests ▾"
  showing, English needs about 292 px, so it scrolls only at 240 px.
- **Links:** `?tab=dms` (old links and bookmarks) opens 전체, so nothing 404s. `?tab=trina|requests` still picks that
  filter, and the "⋯" chip shows its name.
- **The server stays as it is:** `ActivityTab` 'dms' and `POST /api/notifications/read {tab:'dms'}` remain for older
  windows. Nothing on the new screen calls them.

### 2. Read and unread: an "Unreads" switch replaces the "읽음" section

- The list is **newest first**, read and unread together. That is how the phone page and Slack already work.
  - An unread row has its name in semibold and a dot at the right.
  - A read row has normal weight and no dot.
  - There is no "읽음" header and no fold.
- The header has an **"안 읽음 / Unreads" switch**, a pill toggle with `aria-pressed`. It uses the phone's existing
  word, `feed.unreads`. When it is on, only unread rows show.
  - It is off by default.
  - It is kept per browser (`localStorage`, in try/catch). Someone who prefers the inbox-zero view keeps it.
- A row read while the list is open stays where it is until the list opens again or the switch is flipped (the existing
  `wasUnread` logic). Nothing jumps out from under the pointer.
- **Why not keep "unread first"?** The section was what made unread-first readable, and it is the thing that looks
  busy. With the switch, the person picks: everything by time, or only what is new. The rail number still says how
  many are unread.

### 3. Two lines per row

- **Conversation row** (mention or thread reply):
  - Line 1: **who**, then **where** in faint text (`#마케팅 › 스레드`, a DM's name, a chat's title), then the **time**
    at the right edge (the row's tag position).
  - Line 2: the **message preview, one line**, cut with "…". It is ink while unread and muted when read.
  - When line 1 is too long, *where* is cut first, then *who*. The time is never cut.
- **App notice row** (트리나, 요청, joins, shares): the kind's icon, then the **text up to two lines**, then the time at
  the right of line 1. These rows have no other line, so two lines carry the meaning.
- **Photo:** stays 28 px on a computer (`size-list-face`), centered on the two lines. A phone keeps its own tokens.
- **Height:** about 44 px for a two-line row, down from up to 58 px. At 1440, about 30% more rows fit in the same
  height.
- No new "dense/detailed" setting. We have one layout, which is the dense one.

### 4. Header and gear

- The header has: **활동**, then the **안 읽음** switch, then **"Mark all as read" (✓✓)** only while the current filter
  has something unread (as today).
- **The gear leaves the panel.** Slack has no gear in Activity either. "알림 설정" is reached:
  - from Settings › Notifications, as now;
  - from the main area beside the list when no notice is open (`ActivityOpened`, `activity-pick`). That area is
    empty and wide, and it gets a quiet link, "Notification settings".
- On the phone page, the gear stays in its tools row, because the phone has no main area to hold the link.

## Edge cases

- **Empty states.** All of these keep the existing "New messages are in Chats." link when 대화 has unread messages.
  - 전체 with nothing in it: "No notifications yet." (as now).
  - A filter with nothing in it: "Nothing here." (as now).
  - 안 읽음 on with nothing unread: a new line, en "You're all caught up." and ko "모두 읽었어요.", plus a text button
    "Show all" that turns the switch off.
- **Paging.** With 안 읽음 on, the AUTO_PAGES rule still reads older pages until the unread rows are loaded (as today).
  "Load more" stays at the bottom.
- **The 240 px panel in es and id.** The chip row may scroll sideways a few pixels. It never wraps, and the selected
  chip is scrolled into view. At 1440 everything fits in all six languages. Check this with a shot per locale.
- **Phone, 390 px.** Three tabs in a grid. Every control stays 44 px. The rows use the same two-line layout with the
  phone's sizes. The search and day headers stay as they are.
- **Keyboard.**
  - Tab order: the 안 읽음 switch, ✓✓, the chips (each a button with `aria-pressed`, as now), "⋯", then the rows.
  - "⋯" is the shared `MenuButton`: arrow keys move, Enter picks, and Esc closes and returns focus to the chip.
  - The switch's name is "Unreads" with its pressed state, and a screen reader hears "Show unread only".
  - A selected row keeps `aria-current`. No new shortcuts in this change.
- **Touch screens.** Chips and "⋯" keep the 44 px tap box (`pointer-coarse` ::after) and the 44 px minimum width.
- **Member with no 요청.** The "⋯" menu holds 전체 and 트리나. It stays, so the row looks the same for everyone.
- **Mark all as read.** It works as today: shown under 전체, 멘션 and 스레드 while something there is unread, and not
  under 트리나 or 요청.
- **What the gear held.** It was only a link to Settings › Notifications: per-kind switches, room defaults, thread
  replies, quiet hours and sound. That page is unchanged here. UX package D (one set of notification levels) is still
  pending and owns that page. No package D document was found on origin/dev. This plan does not touch notification
  levels.

## Self-review: a non-developer who knows Slack

Read as someone who uses Slack every day and has never seen this code:

1. **"Where did DM go? Slack has DMs in Activity."** Slack lists *every* DM message in Activity. We don't; those are
   in 대화 with their own count. Our DM chip only re-showed mentions and thread replies. Fix: no DM chip, and the
   "New messages are in Chats." link already appears when 대화 has unread messages. No other explanation is needed.
2. **"Two ⋯ buttons?"** An early draft put the gear and "Mark all" into a header "⋯" as well. Two "⋯" side by side,
   one for filters and one for actions, is confusing. Fix: the header has no "⋯". It has the 안 읽음 switch and ✓✓.
   The only "⋯" is in the chip row and is drawn as a chip, so it reads as "more filters".
3. **"I picked 요청 and now I can't find my way back."** Fix: the "⋯" chip turns into "요청 ▾" (pressed), and 전체 is
   always visible as its own chip, one tap away.
4. **"Is 안 읽음 a filter like 멘션?"** In Slack, Unreads is both a filter and a switch. Here it is only a switch, in a
   different place (the header) and with a different shape (a pill switch, not a chip). That way it combines with
   멘션 or 스레드, such as "unread mentions". This is the same as the phone page today.
5. **"Where are notification settings?"** Slack users look under Preferences, and that path still works (Settings ›
   Notifications). The empty main area also links there. Moving the gear has a risk, though: an owner who used it
   daily may miss it. This is an open decision (below).
6. **"What is 트리나 here?"** In a filter menu, "트리나" alone is vague. Fix: the menu item reads "Done by Trina"
   (ko "트리나가 한 일"); the pressed chip shows the short "트리나 ▾". "요청 / Requests" stays as it is: its rows carry
   the 🔌 icon and say what is asked.
7. **"One-line previews hide the message."** The full message is one click away, and the open notice shows on the
   right. Slack's dense layout makes the same trade.

## Open decisions (each with one recommendation)

1. **The gear: remove it from the panel, or keep it?** Recommendation: remove it. It is a link to a page reachable from
   Settings, and Slack has none. The empty main area gets the link.
2. **The 안 읽음 switch's default.** Recommendation: off (everything, newest first). Remember the person's choice in
   this browser.
3. **On the phone, do 트리나 and 요청 also get a "⋯"?** Recommendation: no. The phone keeps three tabs. Requests on a
   phone are rare and sit under 전체.
4. **"⋯" with an icon only, or the word "더 보기"?** Recommendation: the icon, drawn as a chip, with the label "More
   filters". The word does not fit at 240 px in English with the other three chips.
5. **Order inside 안 읽음 off: newest first, or unread first without a header?** Recommendation: newest first. It
   matches the phone page and Slack, and it is what makes the switch meaningful.
6. **Whether this waits for UX package D.** Recommendation: don't wait. This plan changes only the list's controls and
   rows. Package D owns the notification levels and the settings page, and the two don't overlap.
