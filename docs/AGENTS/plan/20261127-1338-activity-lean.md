## Goal

The owner, looking at the 활동 panel: "야 이거 너무 번잡하긴 하네. 이거 슬랙이랑 똑같이 갈려고 했는데.. 어카지?"

The panel next to the rail is 240 px wide on a window up to 1200 px, 256 px at 1280 and 288 px at 1440
(`PanelFrame`: `clamp(240px, min(18rem, 20vw), 300px)`). The chip row inside it has **216 px** at 240 (the panel's
`px-2` and the chip row's own `px-1`). Today it shows:

- a header with the title, "Mark all as read" (only while unread) and a gear that links to Settings › Notifications;
- six chips, 전체 · DM · 멘션 · 스레드 · 트리나 · 요청, that wrap onto two lines at both widths (`flex-wrap`);
- the unread rows, then a folding "읽음" section header with the read rows under it;
- conversation rows of up to four lines: a 28 px photo, "name: message" that may wrap to two lines, then
  "#place › 스레드 · time" (`ListRow` `wrap` + `meta`).

The goal is a panel that a person who knows Slack reads at a glance: one filter line, one way to hide read items, and
rows of two lines. It keeps the "One Activity" decision
([20261126-one-activity.md](../../decisions/20261126-one-activity.md)): every notice stays in 활동, and only the
controls get lighter.

## What the chips hold today (origin/dev 23daebcff, checked in the code)

- **DM** (`activityTabHas(item, 'dms')`): a conversation row whose room is a DM or a group chat. The server lists a
  room row only when it is a mention (`room.mention`) or a reply in a thread I follow (`rooms/inbox.ts` `LISTED_SQL`).
  A mention row is under 멘션 (`code === 'room.mention'`), and a row in a thread carries `room.thread`, so it is under
  스레드. **So no row is only under DM**: the chip is a narrower slice of 멘션 and 스레드, and it is usually empty, which is
  why it has its own empty text (`feed.dmsEmpty`). A plain DM message is never a row; it counts on 대화.
- ⚠ **The owner asked for the DM tab back** on 2026-11-13 ("Activity: the DMs tab is back", docs/DECISIONS.md), the same
  day it was removed (#635). Taking it off the screen again reverses the owner's own call, so it is open decision 1, not
  a silent removal.
- **트리나** (`chipOf`, codes `schedule.success`, `schedule.failure`, `dev_env.job`, `dev_env.box`): what the AI did by
  itself (a scheduled task's run, a project's job). The label is `aiName(t)` (트리나 in the Trina edition, the product's
  short name in the core edition).
- **요청** (`chipOf`, 11 codes: `skill.suggested`, `integration.request`, `onboarding.ai_request`, `service.request`,
  `account.deletion_request`, `drive.share_reconnect`, `integration.template_reconnect`, `connection.reconnect`,
  `skills.merge_waiting`, `file.access_requested`, `access.requested`): something waits for my answer or my hand. A team
  template that no longer applies to me (`templateLeft`) is not a request. Owners and admins always see the chip; a
  member sees it only while an ask to open a file, chat or project of theirs is listed (`chipsFor`).
- Everything else (joins, shares, an added project, a change to the team's AI) is under 전체 only.

## Decisions in this plan

### 1. One filter line that never wraps

The visible chips are **전체 · 멘션 · 스레드**, then **"⋯"**, a chip with the dots icon, tooltip and name "More
filters" (`feed.moreFilters`). Its menu (the shared `components/rooms/MenuButton.tsx`, with a check on the chosen one)
lists **DM** (open decision 1), **트리나가 한 일 / Done by {name}** and, when that person has it, **요청**. 전체 is never
in the menu: it is always a visible chip. Every person's menu has at least two items (DM and 트리나), so the "⋯" looks
the same for everyone.

- **A picked menu filter shows as its own pressed chip, just before "⋯"** (`[요청] (⋯)`), and "⋯" stays "⋯". The person
  sees which filter is on, 전체 is one tap away, and the same "⋯" opens the other menu filters. (The first draft turned
  "⋯" itself into "요청 ▾"; then the way to 트리나 hides inside a chip named 요청. Open decision 2.)
- **A dot on a chip** (as the phone's tabs have) marks something unread under 멘션 or 스레드, and on "⋯" something unread
  under a menu filter. A pending request is never hidden by the menu: "⋯" shows the dot, and the menu item has it too.
- **It never wraps, and never hides "⋯".** Measured in Chromium with the app's font stack (13 px, medium):

  | Row at 240 (216 px today) | ko | en | es | id | ja |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 전체 · 멘션 · 스레드 · ⋯, today's chip padding | 186 | 231 | 237 | 244 | 242 |
  | the same, chips `px-2` and no extra `px-1` on the row (224 px room) | 174 | 219 | 225 | 232* | 230* |
  | with a picked "Requests" chip as well | 229 | 297 | 314 | 323 | 306 |

  (*id and ja fit the 224 px only because they lose 12 px of padding; touch screens add a 44 px minimum per chip.) So
  sideways scrolling, as the first draft proposed, would push "⋯" and its request dot off the edge in every language
  but Korean. Instead:
  - chips get `px-2` (8 px) and the row loses its extra `px-1`;
  - **"⋯" is pinned at the right end, outside the chips' strip**;
  - **chips that do not fit move into the "⋯" menu**, last first (스레드, then 멘션; 전체 and a picked chip never move),
    measured with a `ResizeObserver` on the row. This also covers part 5, where the panel can be any width from 240 to
    400 px.
- **Links:** `?tab=mentions|threads|trina|requests` work as today. `?tab=dms` keeps working whichever way decision 1
  goes: with DM in the menu it picks it; with DM gone, `chipOfParam('dms')` returns 'all'. Nothing else links to
  `?tab=dms` (no server, push or desktop link on origin/dev); only bookmarks and the rail's remembered address can hold
  it, and both pass through `chipOfParam`.
- **The server stays as it is:** `ActivityTab` 'dms' and `POST /api/notifications/read {tab:'dms'}` remain.
- **Mark all as read** works as today: under 전체, DM, 멘션 and 스레드 (the server reads the tab), never under 트리나 or 요청.

### 2. Read and unread: an "안 읽음 / Unreads" switch replaces the "읽음" section

- The list is **newest first**, read and unread together, as the phone page shows it and Slack does.
  - An unread row has its first line in semibold and a dot at the right; a read row has normal weight and no dot.
  - There is no "읽음" header and no fold (`PanelSection` `activity:read` goes; its kept fold state is harmless).
- The header has an **"안 읽음" switch**: a pill toggle with `aria-pressed`, the phone's word `feed.unreads`. When it is
  on, only unread rows show.
  - Off by default. Remembered in this browser (`localStorage`, read and written in try/catch). The phone's switch
    (today a plain `useState`) uses the same key, so the two never disagree in one browser.
  - With the switch on, a row read while the list is open stays on screen until the list opens again or the switch is
    flipped (the existing `wasUnread` set now does this job). Nothing jumps out from under the pointer.
- **Paging:** `AUTO_PAGES` (reading older pages by themselves until every unread row is loaded) runs only while the
  switch is on. With newest first and the switch off, there is no reason to fetch nine pages ahead.
- **Why not keep "unread first"?** The section was what made unread-first readable, and it is the thing that looks busy.
  With the switch the person picks: everything by time, or only what is new. The rail number still says how many.
- **Header room at 240:** title + switch + ✓✓ fit at 1052–1200 in every language. Below 1052 the header also has
  "목록 숨기기" (32 px), and then Indonesian (title 70 + "Belum dibaca" 106 + 32 + 32) and Japanese (106 + 46 + 32 + 32)
  run 12–36 px over 216. There the switch drops to its bell icon, with the word as its tooltip and accessible name (a
  container query on the header). The title never shrinks to "Ak…".

### 3. Two lines per row

- **Conversation row** (mention, thread reply, an AI chat's thread):
  - Line 1: **where**, then the **time** at the right edge (`tag`). Where = `#마케팅 › 스레드`, a group chat's name, a
    shared AI chat's title. Semibold in ink while unread.
  - Line 2: **who: message**, one line, cut with "…". The message is ink while unread and muted when read.
  - A 1:1 DM: line 1 is the person's name, and line 2 drops the "who:" (it would repeat the name).
  - Why where first (open decision 3): at 240 a row has about 146 px for words and time. With "who · where" on line 1
    (the first draft), "Minji Kim" leaves about 20 px for the place, so the place is lost on most rows. Where first
    keeps both: the place on line 1, the name at the start of line 2, and the photo shows who as well.
- **App notice row** (트리나, 요청, joins, shares): the kind's icon, the **text up to two lines**, the time as the tag on
  line 1. A notice that names a chat (`chat.shared`, `chat.lost`, `chat.left`) keeps that title: the text is one line
  and the title is line 2 in faint text (today it is in `meta`; the first draft dropped it).
- **Photo:** stays 28 px (`size-list-face`), centered on the two lines.
- **Height:** about 44 px for a two-line row, down from 58–76 px. At 1440, about 30% more rows fit.
- No "dense/detailed" setting. We have one layout, the dense one.
- `ListRow` has one `name` node and a `tag`; line 1 = name (where) + tag (time), line 2 = `meta` with `wrap` off. If
  `meta` cannot hold "who: message" cut at the end, add a `metaKeep`-like slot rather than custom markup.

### 4. Header and gear

- The header has: **활동**, the **안 읽음** switch, and **✓✓ Mark all as read** only while the current filter has
  something unread (as today).
- **The gear leaves the computer's panel** (open decision 4). It is only a link to Settings › Notifications
  (`data-testid="activity-panel-settings"`; no test clicks it, the phone tests click `activity-settings-phone`). It is
  reached:
  - from Settings › Notifications, as now;
  - from the main area beside the list when no notice is open (`ActivityOpened`, `activity-pick`): a quiet link,
    "Notification settings" (`feed.settings`), under the hint.
- The phone page keeps its gear in its tools row (no main area there).

### 5. The list panel can be resized (its own PR, all five panels)

The owner: the list panel is fixed at about 288 px, so it looks wide on a 13" MacBook and narrow on a 16" one. Slack
lets you drag its sidebar.

- **Which panel:** the list panel beside the rail, in every place (트리나, 대화, 활동, 프로젝트, 파일): they are all
  `PanelFrame`, and one width for all of them means switching places never makes the list jump.
- **Default:** today's width (`clamp(240px, min(18rem, 20vw), 300px)`) while nothing is remembered. Someone who never
  drags sees no change.
- **Min 240 px** (today's minimum; the 1052 px fold rule and the chip row are built on it). **Max 400 px**, and never so
  wide that the main area gets less than **740 px** (the room with its thread, the same 740 as `PANEL_ALWAYS_PX` =
  72 + 240 + 740). So at a 1052 px window there is nothing to drag; at 1280 the panel can reach 400.
- **Remembered per device in px** (`localStorage` `mycel.listPanel.width`, try/catch), not as a fraction like the
  right-hand panels: the point is "my width on this screen". A narrower window clamps it for the time being and gives it
  back when the window is wider again.
- **Double-click** the divider: back to the default, and forget the width.
- **Keyboard:** the divider is a focusable `role="separator"` with `aria-orientation="vertical"`, `aria-valuenow/min/max`
  and a name ("Resize the list", new key `rail.resizeList`). **Right arrow widens, Left narrows** (24 px; Shift 96 px),
  Home is the smallest, End the largest. Phones have no divider (no panel). When the panel is folded or hidden
  (`railPanel.ts`), there is no divider.
- **Fit with the existing code** (checked): `PanelDivider` and `lib/panelWidth.ts` are built for a panel on the right of
  its column, and need four changes to serve a panel on the left:
  1. the divider sits on the panel's **right** edge (`-right-px`, not `-left-px`);
  2. the width is `x − panel.left`, not `panel.right − x`, and the arrow keys swap (`keyWidth` takes the side);
  3. the column beside it is the panel's **next** sibling (`<main>`; `RailColumns` renders the rail and the panel as a
     fragment, so the panel's next sibling is `<main>`), not the previous one;
  4. it remembers px, not a fraction (`settlePanel`/`fitPanel` get a px mode).
  Add these as a `side: 'start' | 'end'` and `remember: 'px' | 'fraction'` on `PanelSizing` (a new
  `LIST_PANEL: { key, min: 240, otherMin: 740, side: 'start', remember: 'px', max: 400 }`), so the right-hand panels keep
  their behaviour and their tests (`panel-width.test.ts`, `panel-divider.test.tsx`). `PanelFrame` becomes `relative`
  with `w-[var(--panel-w,clamp(240px,min(18rem,20vw),300px))]`.
- **Two knock-on fixes:**
  - `railPanel.ts` `PANEL_ALWAYS_BOTH_PX` (1448 = 88 + **300** + 1060) assumes the panel is at most 300 px. With a wider
    panel, a room with its thread and side chat would not fit. The fold rule must use the panel's real width
    (rail + panel + 1060), kept in the `useRailPanel` store as it changes.
  - The right-hand panel's divider re-fits only when the window or the row changes size. Widening the list shrinks
    `<main>` without that, so the side panel could leave the chat under its 360 px. `PanelDivider` also watches its
    other column with the `ResizeObserver` it already uses.
- **Tests:** unit for the px mode and the left side in `panel-width.test.ts`; `panel-divider.test.tsx` for the keys on
  a left panel; e2e at 1440: drag, reload keeps it, double-click resets, keyboard, a 1052 window clamps it, a 1448 window
  with thread + side chat folds the list when the panel is wide.

## Edge cases

- **Empty states.** All keep the "New messages are in Chats." link when 대화 has unread messages.
  - 전체 with nothing: "No notifications yet." (as now). A filter with nothing: "Nothing here." (as now; DM keeps
    `feed.dmsEmpty` while it exists).
  - 안 읽음 on with nothing unread: new en "You're all caught up." / ko "모두 읽었어요.", and a text button "Show all"
    that turns the switch off.
- **Keyboard.** Tab order: the 안 읽음 switch, ✓✓, the chips (buttons with `aria-pressed`, as now), "⋯", the rows.
  "⋯" is `MenuButton`: arrows move, Enter picks, Esc closes and returns focus. The switch's name is "Unreads" with its
  pressed state. A selected row keeps `aria-current`.
- **Touch screens.** Chips and "⋯" keep the 44 px tap box (`pointer-coarse` ::after) and 44 px minimum width; the
  overflow rule measures with it, so on a touch laptop more chips go into "⋯".
- **A filter the person loses** (요청 for a member whose last request is answered) falls back to 전체, as today.
- **What the gear held.** Only the link. UX package D (one set of notification levels) owns Settings › Notifications and
  is untouched here; no package D document was found on origin/dev.

## Steps (when it is built)

1. `lib/activityKinds.ts`: `VISIBLE_CHIPS` (all, mentions, threads) and `MORE_CHIPS` (dms per decision 1, trina,
   requests); `chipOfParam('dms')` per decision 1.
2. `ActivityPanel.tsx`: the chip row (no wrap, `px-2` chips, pinned "⋯" `MenuButton` with `dot`, overflow by
