# One image viewer: a picture opens full-window, like Slack, not small in the side panel

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-09 08:17
- Branch: `feat/image-viewer`
- Status: done
- Where: trina-ax core (`web/src/`: a new viewer and its list, the places that open a picture). Why: rooms, chat and
  file previews are core (see docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md)
- Version impact: minor. A visible UX change (a picture opens in a full-window viewer); no API, setting or data change.

## Goal

A picture clicked in a room opens in the right-hand panel, small and with no zoom: a screenshot of a web page is hard
to read. The owner wants pictures to open like Slack, across the app: the whole window, zoom, next/previous.

## Slack's image viewer: what is known

- Slack added zoom and pan to its image viewer "so you can look over designs in greater detail" ([Slack blog, 31 July
  2018](https://slack.com/blog/productivity/in-case-you-missed-it-new-emoji-improved-search-and-more)).
- Mattermost users asked for "Copy / Zoom on image in preview (same as Slack / Skype)"
  ([Mattermost feature request](https://mattermost.uservoice.com/forums/306457-general/suggestions/39999706-copy-zoom-on-image-in-preview-same-as-slack-s)).
- On phones a picture opens full screen; ⋯ › Save Image (iOS) or Download (Android) saves it
  ([Slack help, Add files to Slack](https://slack.com/help/articles/201330736-Add-files-to-Slack)). Slack's
  [Android release notes](https://slack.com/release-notes/android) list fixes to zooming pictures.
- Slack documents nothing else about the viewer. The top bar (name, sender, time), click-to-zoom, ←/→, Esc, a backdrop
  click to close and swipe-down come from the owner's brief. They were not checked in a running Slack: this task signs
  in to no outside account.

Deliberately different: no comment, share or ⋯ menu in the bar (lean); the list is only the pictures loaded on screen,
not the channel's whole file history; no "open in a new tab" in the desktop app or the phone app (it would open the
system browser, which has no session; `desktop/src/main/security.ts:131`); left and right swipes step on phones.

## Design

- **Parts.** `web/src/lib/imageViewer.ts`: the open state (a small external store like `lib/sidePanel.ts`) and the list
  ordering. `web/src/lib/zoom.ts`: pure zoom and pan math. `web/src/components/ImageViewer.tsx`: the overlay,
  `<ImageGroup>` and `useViewerItem()`. The host mounts once beside `DialogHost` (`App.tsx:249`) and renders in a portal
  on `document.body`: the team page itself, never a frame. A route change closes it.
- **Opening and the list.** A conversation pane wraps itself in `<ImageGroup>`: the room timeline, the thread panel, the
  AI chat's messages and the composer's picture row. Each picture registers its item (source, name, sender, time, its
  element) through `useViewerItem`. A click opens the group's items in DOM order (`compareDocumentPosition`): that is
  message order, and only what is on screen. Pictures hidden by access rules (locked, withheld, unavailable) render no
  picture, so they are never in the list. The list follows the group while open (a new picture adds to "3 / 8"). A
  picture outside any group opens alone.
- **Addresses.** Only the existing ones, with their checks:
  - room picture: thumbnail `roomFileUrl(…,{thumb})`, original `roomFileUrl(…)` (inline). Membership and message
    visibility are checked on every read. Download is `?download=1`.
  - chat attachment: thumbnail `thumbnailUrl(id)`, original `fileUrl(path,'inline')` (the team-file check by path),
    HEIC/HEIF through `/api/files/preview`. Download is `fileUrl(path)`.
  - answer picture: the `<img>` there is already the original (`fileUrl(path,'inline')`), so it shows at once.
  - composer: the `File`'s object URL.
  - Over 20 MB (the panel's image limit, `previewLimit`): the thumbnail, a note and the download.
- **Loading.** The thumbnail sits under the original (`pointer-events: none`, `aria-hidden`) and is **removed** once the
  original loads. Only then is the original the one `<img>` under the pointer, and nothing paints behind a transparent
  picture (#231): it sits on the dark backdrop, with no checkerboard.
- **Zoom and pan: hand-written** with Pointer Events and CSS `transform` on one element. The math is small and pure
  (fit = min(1, stage/natural), so a fitted picture is never upscaled; zoom about a point; clamp the pan, centring an axis
  that fits). It is unit-tested. A library (react-zoom-pan-pinch, yet-another-react-lightbox, PhotoSwipe; all MIT,
  roughly 10–25 kB gzipped) would bring its own DOM, styles and data model to bend to the app's bar, i18n, focus trap
  and the one-`<img>` rule for copying.
  - A click toggles fit ↔ 100% (2× fit for a picture already shown at 80% or more), centred on the click point. A drag
    of 4 px or more is a pan, not a click.
  - ⌘/Ctrl + wheel and trackpad pinch (Chromium sends wheel + ctrlKey; Safari sends `gesture*`) zoom about the pointer.
    The listener is not passive, so the page itself never zooms.
  - +/−/0 keys; −, %, + in the bar. The zoom runs from fit to 400%.
- **Phones.** Pinch about the midpoint, double-tap to toggle at the tap, one-finger pan when zoomed, swipe down to close
  when not zoomed (the picture follows the finger and the backdrop fades; close past 20% of the height or on a flick).
  A tap shows or hides the bar and the arrows. `touch-action: none` on the stage.
- **Bar.** The name; the sender's picture and name (`Avatar`, or `AiFace` for the AI); the time; "3 / 7"; zoom −/%/+;
  download; open the original in a new tab (plain browsers only); ✕. It is always visible on computers.
- **Desktop "이미지 복사".** The viewer is on the top frame and the original is the topmost `<img>`, so Electron's
  `copyImageAt` copies the original. 이미지 저장… saves it (a same-origin address, `saveableImage`).
- **Focus and screen readers.** `role="dialog"`, `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-label` "이미지 보기: {name}". The app's
  `useFocusTrap` keeps Tab inside and handles Esc. Focus returns to the opening picture, found again by its key if the
  list re-rendered. The name and the count are announced politely on a step. A click on the backdrop closes the viewer.
  The page behind does not scroll.
- **Reduced motion.** Fades and the zoom ease use CSS transitions, which index.css already turns off. The swipe-close
  snaps back.
- **Words** in ko, en, id, ja, es. Existing keys are reused where they fit (`common.close`, `common.download`).

## Decisions (coordinator, 2026-11-09)

1. Files, FileView, links and sources keep the side panel. The picture the panel shows opens the viewer with that picture
   alone; the panel and its actions stay.
2. Next/previous covers the pane that was clicked: the timeline (its pictures, replies also sent to the room included),
   the thread panel (that thread's root and replies), the AI chat (the visible branch, attachments and answer pictures in
   order), a composer's row (its pictures). It never crosses into another thread or loads older messages.
3. The room composer's picture tiles open the viewer too, like the AI chat's: both boxes alike.
4. Pictures outside conversations open the viewer with that picture alone.
5. Left and right swipes step on phones.
6. **Back closes the viewer.** Opening pushes one history entry with the same address (`history.pushState`, the current
   state copied, so React Router's `idx`, `key` and `usr` stay as they were and its back helpers and deep links are
   untouched); a `popstate` that leaves that entry closes the viewer. Closing with ✕, Esc, the backdrop or a swipe calls
   `history.back()` when that entry is still the current one, so none is left behind. A route change while it is open
   (a notice) closes it without going back; Back from that page skips the entry it left. The browser Back, Android's
   back (the phone app's WebView `goBack`, `mobile/App.tsx:403`) and iOS's edge swipe all land on that `popstate`.

## Risks

- Copying needs the original to be the only picture under the pointer: the e2e checks the right-clicked element.
- A HEIC sent in a room has no drawable original in Chrome; it stays on its 640 px thumbnail, with the download.
- Back: React Router re-renders on that `popstate` with the same location key (a POP), so effects keyed on the pathname or
  the key do not run again. Left over: after a reload with the viewer open, one Back lands on the same page again (the
  viewer's entry); no address changes.

## Result

Built as planned with the decisions above (docs/DECISIONS.md "One image viewer"); PR
https://github.com/trina-app/trina-ax/pull/246. What differs or was found:
- One more picture than the inventory listed: the AI chat composer's tile opens the viewer from the file's own bytes
  until it is sent; a HEIC one, once uploaded, opens as the server's JPEG.
- The backdrop is opaque: at 95 % the room's text still read through on a phone. The page shows only while a swipe
  down is closing the viewer.
- A click on a picture already shown at 80 % or more goes to 2× the fitted size, not 100 %: 100 % would hardly change
  it. A large screenshot (the owner's case) goes to 100 %.
- The focus starts on the picture's area, not on a bar button, so ← → and + − 0 work at once and no button ring shows.
- No library: the zoom and pan are about 100 lines of pure functions (lib/zoom.ts), unit-tested.
- The room's side chat with the AI (merged into dev meanwhile) is its own pane, like the AI chat.
- The phone app: the viewer's download is the panel's (`startDownload` → `downloadToApp`: the page fetches the file and
  hands it to the app, which opens the share sheet on iOS and Android); the new-tab button is not shown there.
- After the review: each showing draws its pictures anew (the same picture twice in a pane stayed loading); a click or a
  swipe closes when nothing could be drawn; a quick close and reopen waits for the first back to land; Back skips the
  entry a page change left behind; the page's ⌘K / ⌘⇧O wait while it is open; Tab from the picture's area reaches the
  bar; a shared chat's question by a gone member is not shown as mine; a Mac's control-click is left to the menu.
