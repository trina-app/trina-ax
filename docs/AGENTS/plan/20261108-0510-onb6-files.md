## Goal

The owner's 팀 파일 step today uploads each hand-picked file at once. Most owners have their company documents in a
folder and do not know which ones the team needs. v6 (onboarding plan §3): the owner picks a folder, the page lists
its documents without reading them, the AI picks from names, paths, types, sizes and dates only, the owner confirms
one checked list, and only then do the files go up. Never fully automatic; no file content leaves before the upload.

## Risks

- Migration 272 (checked against `origin/dev` before the PR). Additive: an older build runs a leftover rank chat as an
  ordinary chat; rank chats live at most 30 s and are purged.
- File names and folder paths of the owner's computer go to the AI (the person pressed [폴더 고르기]; the hint says
  so). The rank chat is the caller's own, never listed, purged after its answer; the entries are capped and cut.
- The AI sees entries as data only: no tool, no team files, no rules, no skills; names are tag-broken.
- The five app languages; phone (no folders) and desktop (`webkitdirectory`).
