# A seal lands on the sign mark, whatever text the AI names

- Date (UTC): 2026-11-23 16:54
- Branch: `fix/seal-on-mark`
- Status: in progress
- Where: Team AX core (`server/src/files`, `web/src`). Why: both document editors and the file preview are core (see docs/REPOSITORY-RULES.md)
- Version impact: patch, a seal now lands where the person asked, no new capability

## Goal

In real runs on Word, 2 of 4 seals landed about 10 mm left of "(인)", because the AI named the whole line and the seal
was centred on all of it (QA TC-8357). A seal off its mark is a visible failure on a signed form. The panel preview also
draws floating seals about 4 mm too low (QA TC-8358).

## Scope

In: one shared helper that narrows an `over` label to its last sign mark, used by edit_office and edit_hwp; a refusal when
a label holds several marks and no `occurrence`; result text that names the text the picture went on; the preview's
vertical position of floating pictures placed relative to their line.
Out: `after`, `before`, `next_to` and cell placement; labels with no sign mark (as today); other floating pictures in
the preview.

## Steps

1. `server/src/files/sign-mark.ts`: find the marks, choose the target, or say which one is needed.
2. Word `over` and HWP `at` (place over) use it. "(서명 또는 인)" aims at the seal word.
3. The result names the text the picture was placed on.
4. Preview: floating pictures with `relativeFrom="line"` align to the line's top.
5. Tests for both tools; a real tool-path pass on Word, .hwp and .hwpx, opened in Hancom Docs.

A label that holds a mark now lands on the mark, not on the label's centre. No migration, permission or route change.
