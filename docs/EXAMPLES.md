# Team workflow examples

[English catalog](../examples/README.md) · [한국어 예제](../examples/README.ko.md)

Copyable skills for CEO/team lead, sales, marketing, finance, HR and meetings live in `examples/skills/`. The catalog gives fictional inputs, actual installation paths, permission requirements and human review steps. Skills are reusable instructions, not trained models or additional access.

[Try the files without an AI account](QUICKSTART.md). For a real AI run, connect only an authorized provider account. Human review remains necessary before sharing, sending or approving outputs.
