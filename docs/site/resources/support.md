## Get help

| You need | Go to |
|---|---|
| Hosting, plans and pricing | [Current plans](https://ax.trina.app/#pricing), or email contact@trina.app |
| Hosted customer support | contact@trina.app |
| How to use Trina AX | [Team guide](../../for-teams.md), then [ask a question](https://github.com/trina-app/trina-ax/issues/new/choose) |
| Installing or operating | [IT guide](../../for-admins.md) and [Troubleshooting](../operate/troubleshooting.md) |
| A bug or an idea | [Issue forms](https://github.com/trina-app/trina-ax/issues/new/choose) |
| Security or private information | The [security policy](../../../SECURITY.md). Email privately, never in a public issue. |

Public reports are welcome in English and Korean.

:::warning
Never post customer documents, real names, account details, keys, tokens, setup or invite links, cookies, databases or raw logs. Reproduce with fictional data.
:::
